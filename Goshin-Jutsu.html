Introduction

What is Kodokan Goshin-jutsu?

Kodokan Goshin-jutsu (講道館護身術) was established in 1956 by a designated Kodokan study committee (incorporating master judoka alongside aikido and jujutsu authorities like Kenji Tomiki). It updated classical self-defense (Kime-no-Kata) to address modern physical assaults.

The kata is composed of 21 techniques arranged into two main divisions: Unarmed Attacks (Toshu no bu — 12 techniques) and Armed Attacks (Buki no bu — 9 techniques).

The armed division addresses realistic attacks using three weapons: the knife/dagger (Tantō), the wooden staff/stick (Jō), and the pistol/firearm (Kenjū).

Technical execution emphasizes prompt preemptive or reactive body evasion (Tai-sabaki), precise decisive counter-striking (Atemi-waza) to vital points (Kyusho), joint locks (Kansetsu-waza), takedowns, and secure weapon disarms without hesitating.

Structure

The Five Sets (21 Techniques)

The kata progresses systematically from close-quarter grappling defenses and striking attacks to blade, stick, and firearm threats.

SET 01 — UNARMED

Kumitsukareta baai

組み付かれた場合 — When Held / Close Range

Defenses against close-range grabs, collar seizures, chokes, and bear hugs using wrist locks, atemi, and hip throws.

  • 1. Ryote-dori Both Wrists Seized
  • 2. Hidari-eri-dori Left Lapel Seized
  • 3. Migi-eri-dori Right Lapel Seized
  • 4. Kataude-dori Single Arm Seized
  • 5. Ushiro-eri-dori Collar from Behind
  • 6. Ushiro-jime Rear Choke
  • 7. Kakae-dori Rear Bear Hug
SET 02 — UNARMED

Hanarete iru baai

離れている場合 — At a Distance / Strikes

Defenses against incoming punches, diagonal hand strikes, front kicks, and side kicks from a distance.

  • 8. Naname-uchi Slanting Strike
  • 9. Ago-tsuki Uppercut to Chin
  • 10. Gammen-tsuki Punch to Face
  • 11. Mae-geri Front Kick
  • 12. Yoko-geri Side Kick
SET 03 — WEAPONS

Tanto no bu

短刀の部 — Knife Attacks

Disarming and neutralizing thrusts and slashes delivered with a knife/dagger at close and lunging ranges.

  • 13. Tsukkake Direct Thrust (Close)
  • 14. Choku-tsuki Straight Lunge Thrust
  • 15. Naname-tsuki Slanting Knife Thrust
SET 04 — WEAPONS

Jo no bu

杖の部 — Stick / Staff Attacks

Neutralizing overhead swings, downward strikes, and two-handed thrusts using a medium wooden staff.

  • 16. Furiage Raising the Stick
  • 17. Furioroshi Downward Stick Blow
  • 18. Morote-tsuki Two-Hand Stick Thrust
SET 05 — WEAPONS

Kenju no bu

拳銃の部 — Pistol / Firearm Threats

Redirecting the line of fire, disarming, and controlling an assailant holding a handgun at point-blank range.

  • 19. Shomen-zuke Pistol at Abdomen (Front)
  • 20. Koshi-gamae Pistol at Hip Level
  • 21. Haimen-zuke Pistol to the Back

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

Each of the 21 techniques is detailed strictly according to the official Kodokan Judo textbook standard.

01
両手取 Ryote-dori "Both Wrists Seized"

Uke steps forward and grabs both of Tori's wrists from the front. Tori raises the wrists outward to off-balance Uke, delivers a front kick (Mae-geri) to Uke's groin, steps back, and applies a double-wrist lock (Waki-gatame / Kote-gaeshi variation).

1.Uke steps forward grasping both of Tori's wrists firmly from the front.
2.Tori spreads both hands outward while delivering a sharp right front snap kick (Mae-geri) to Uke's testicles (Kinteki).
3.As Uke flinches, Tori steps back with the left foot, twists Uke's right wrist with both hands into an outward lock, and forces Uke down to the mat for submission.
Kodokan Key Point: Immediate atemi — kick immediately on the wrist grab to break Uke's posture before applying the wrist lock.
Unarmed (Close)Groin Kick AtemiWrist Lock
02
左襟取 Hidari-eri-dori "Left Lapel Seized"

Uke grabs Tori's left lapel with the right hand and pushes. Tori covers Uke's hand, strikes Uke's chin with a rising palm heel (Teisho), and rotates Uke's arm into an armlock (Ude-hishigi-waki-gatame) to control Uke face-down.

1.Uke advances and grabs Tori's left lapel with the right hand, applying pushing pressure.
2.Tori covers Uke's right hand with the left hand, steps back slightly, and delivers a rising right palm-heel strike (Teisho-ate) to Uke's chin (Ago).
3.Tori pivots back-left, traps Uke's right extended arm under the left armpit (Waki-gatame), and presses Uke prone onto the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Teisho strike — deliver the palm-heel to the chin to snap the head back before transitioning into Waki-gatame.
Unarmed (Close)Chin Strike (Teisho)Waki-gatame
03
右襟取 Migi-eri-dori "Right Lapel Seized"

Uke grabs Tori's right lapel with the right hand and pulls backward. Tori follows the pull, delivers a right fist strike to Uke's solar plexus, reaches over Uke's right arm, and applies an arm entanglement lock (Ude-hishigi-waki-gatame / Ude-gatame).

1.Uke seizes Tori's right collar with the right hand and pulls backward.
2.Tori steps forward with the right foot following the pull, striking Uke's solar plexus (Suigetsu) with a right fist (Tsuki).
3.Tori threads the left arm over Uke's right elbow, traps Uke's arm against the torso, and leverages the joint downward to bring Uke to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Following the pull — do not pull back; step in with the solar plexus strike in one continuous motion.
Unarmed (Close)Solar Plexus AtemiArm Lock
04
片腕取 Kataude-dori "Single Arm Seized"

Walking side-by-side, Uke suddenly grasps Tori's right wrist with the left hand and grips Tori's right upper arm with the right hand. Tori steps forward, kicks Uke's knee, and pivots to apply a standing armlock (Waki-gatame).

1.Walking forward together, Uke seizes Tori's right wrist and upper arm from the right flank.
2.Tori steps the left foot forward, delivers a sharp left stomping kick (Fumikomi) to Uke's right knee joint, breaking Uke's balance.
3.Tori steps the right foot back, pivots 180 degrees, traps Uke's right arm under the armpit, and forces Uke to the mat in Waki-gatame.
Kodokan Key Point: Knee stamp — stomp the side of Uke's knee joint to disrupt Uke's posture before pivoting into the armlock.
Unarmed (Close)Knee StompStanding Armlock
05
後襟取 Ushiro-eri-dori "Collar Seized from Behind"

Uke seizes Tori's back collar from behind with the right hand and pulls. Tori pivots 180 degrees under the arm, strikes Uke's armpit/ribs, clamps Uke's extended arm, and applies an armlock over the shoulder.

1.Uke approaches from behind and seizes Tori's collar with the right hand, pulling backward.
2.Tori raises the right arm, turns 180 degrees clockwise beneath Uke's right arm, and delivers an elbow strike (Ushiro-ate) to Uke's ribs.
3.Tori locks Uke's right elbow across the shoulder/chest and applies downward leverage to control Uke.
Kodokan Key Point: Spinning under the grip — ducking cleanly beneath Uke's grasping arm without getting tangled.
Unarmed (Close)Rear CollarSpinning Lock
06
後絞 Ushiro-jime "Rear Choke"

Uke wraps the right arm around Tori's neck from behind in a naked strangle (Hadaka-jime). Tori pulls down on Uke's forearm, delivers a rear elbow strike to Uke's ribs, ducks under, and throws Uke with a shoulder throw (Seoi-nage variation).

1.Uke applies a rear choke (Hadaka-jime) from behind with the right forearm across Tori's throat.
2.Tori immediately pulls down on Uke's right arm with both hands to relieve throat pressure, stepping back with the left foot and delivering a left elbow strike (Ushiro-ate) to Uke's solar plexus.
3.Tori steps under Uke's right arm, drops hips, and throws Uke forward over the shoulder (Ippon-seoi-nage).
Kodokan Key Point: Airway relief — pull down firmly on Uke's forearm before delivering the rear elbow blow and entering the throw.
Unarmed (Close)Choke EscapeShoulder Throw
07
抱取 Kakae-dori "Rear Bear Hug"

Uke wraps both arms around Tori's torso from behind, pinning Tori's arms. Tori raises both arms to break the clamp, stomps on Uke's foot, turns inside, and throws Uke with a hip throw (O-goshi / Uki-goshi).

1.Uke wraps both arms around Tori's torso and arms from behind in a tight bear hug.
2.Tori raises both arms forward and upward while stamping the right heel firmly onto Uke's instep (Kori).
3.As Uke loosens the grip, Tori drops the hips, steps the right foot back between Uke's legs, wraps the right arm around Uke's waist, and throws Uke with a large hip throw.
Kodokan Key Point: Instep stamp & arm expansion — simultaneously expand arms and stomp the instep to break the bear hug grip.
Unarmed (Close)Instep StompHip Throw
08
斜打 Naname-uchi "Slanting Strike / Temple Strike"

Uke steps forward with a diagonal chopping fist strike to Tori's left temple. Tori ducks inside, blocks Uke's right arm, delivers a right palm-heel strike to Uke's chin, and throws Uke with Osoto-gari.

1.Uke advances with the right foot delivering a diagonal hammer-fist strike aimed at Tori's left temple.
2.Tori ducks slightly, steps forward-left inside the blow, blocks Uke's forearm with the left hand, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho).
3.Tori steps deep behind Uke's right leg and sweeps Uke to the mat with a powerful Major Outer Reap (Osoto-gari).
Kodokan Key Point: Inside entry & Osoto-gari — enter deep inside the arc of the strike with simultaneous Teisho and reaping leg.
Unarmed (Distance)Temple Strike DefenseOsoto-gari
09
あご突 Ago-tsuki "Uppercut to Chin"

Uke steps in with a right uppercut to Tori's chin. Tori yields the head back, parries Uke's wrist with the left hand, delivers a right palm strike under Uke's chin, and applies a standing wrist twist (Kote-gaeshi).

1.Uke steps forward with the right foot, delivering a sharp right rising punch (Ago-tsuki) toward Tori's jaw.
2.Tori steps back with the left foot, avoids the blow, intercepts Uke's right wrist with the left hand, and strikes Uke's chin with the right palm (Teisho).
3.Tori grips Uke's right wrist with both hands, steps back, and executes an outward wrist turn (Kote-gaeshi) to force Uke onto the back.
Kodokan Key Point: Wrist interception & Kote-gaeshi — redirect the rising uppercut into an immediate rotational wrist lock.
Unarmed (Distance)Uppercut DefenseKote-gaeshi
10
顔面突 Gammen-tsuki "Straight Punch to Face"

Uke steps forward with a straight right punch to Tori's face. Tori steps back-left to parry the punch, steps inside with a right elbow strike to Uke's solar plexus, wraps the arm, and applies an armlock over the shoulder.

1.Uke advances with the right foot, throwing a straight right punch at Tori's face.
2.Tori steps back-left with the left foot, parries Uke's right wrist outward with the left palm, and drives a right elbow strike (Hiji-ate) into Uke's chest/ribs.
3.Tori wraps the right arm over Uke's extended right elbow, pivots, and applies a downward armlock (Ude-gatame / Waki-gatame variation).
Kodokan Key Point: Parrying & inside elbow blow — parry the linear punch while immediately closing distance with the elbow strike.
Unarmed (Distance)Straight Punch DefenseElbow Strike
11
前蹴 Mae-geri "Front Kick"

Uke delivers a right front kick to Tori's groin. Tori steps back-right to evade, scoops Uke's kicking heel with the left hand, lifts the leg, pushes Uke's shoulder, and dumps Uke onto the tatami.

1.Uke steps forward and delivers a forceful right front snap kick (Mae-geri) toward Tori's groin/abdomen.
2.Tori turns the body slightly to the right, steps back with the right foot, and scoops Uke's right ankle/heel from underneath with the left forearm.
3.Tori elevates Uke's trapped leg while stepping forward and pushing Uke's right shoulder with the right palm (Sukui-nage / leg-lift takedown).
Kodokan Key Point: Scooping the kicking leg — turn sideways off the centerline and scoop the heel upward in one smooth motion.
Unarmed (Distance)Front Kick CatchLeg Scoop Takedown
12
横蹴 Yoko-geri "Side Kick"

Uke approaches sideways and delivers a right side kick to Tori's ribs. Tori pivots back-left to evade, scoops Uke's right ankle, hooks Uke's neck with the right arm, and executes an outer leg sweep (Osoto-gari variation).

1.Uke turns sideways and delivers a powerful right side kick (Yoko-geri) aimed at Tori's midsection.
2.Tori steps back-left, avoiding the kick, and wraps the right arm around Uke's extended leg to trap the ankle under the armpit.
3.Tori steps deep behind Uke's supporting left leg with the right foot, pushes Uke's chest/neck, and takes Uke down backward.
Kodokan Key Point: Side kick trap — trap the kicking leg under the armpit and attack the supporting base leg immediately.
Unarmed (Distance)Side Kick CatchSupport Leg Sweep
13
突掛 Tsukkake "Direct Thrust / Quick Knife Stab"

Standing at close distance, Uke reaches into the jacket to draw a knife and stab Tori's stomach. Tori steps in preemptively before the blade clears, controls Uke's drawing hand, strikes Uke's face, and applies a standing armlock.

1.Uke reaches the right hand inside the jacket to draw the concealed dagger (Tantō).
2.Before Uke can draw the blade, Tori steps forward with the right foot, pins Uke's right hand/wrist against Uke's chest with the left hand, and strikes Uke's face with a right palm heel (Teisho).
3.Tori steps back-right, applies Waki-gatame to Uke's right arm, takes Uke to the mat, disarms the dagger, and controls Uke.
Kodokan Key Point: Preemptive block (Sen-no-sen) — trap Uke's drawing hand inside the jacket before the blade is freed.
Knife (Tantō)Preemptive JamWaki-gatame Disarm
14
直突 Choku-tsuki "Straight Knife Thrust"

Uke steps forward in a deep lunge with a straight knife thrust toward Tori's stomach. Tori pivots off the line of attack, traps Uke's right wrist, strikes the elbow, and takes Uke down into an armlock disarm.

1.Uke steps forward forcefully with the right foot, thrusting the dagger straight at Tori's abdomen.
2.Tori pivots back-left (Tai-sabaki) off the line of the blade, parrying Uke's right wrist with the left hand.
3.Tori places the right hand over Uke's right elbow, levers the joint downward in Waki-gatame, brings Uke prone to the mat, and strips the knife.
Kodokan Key Point: Stepping off the line — clear the torso completely from the blade's path while securing the wrist.
Knife (Tantō)Lunge Thrust EvasionWrist Lock Disarm
15
斜突 Naname-tsuki "Slanting Knife Thrust / Temple Slash"

Uke raises the dagger overhead and delivers a slanting downward ice-pick stab at Tori's neck/temple. Tori ducks inside, blocks Uke's wrist, delivers a palm strike to Uke's chin, and executes an outward wrist turn (Kote-gaeshi) to disarm.

1.Uke raises the dagger with a reverse grip and steps forward with a downward diagonal stab toward Tori's left neck.
2.Tori steps forward-left inside the strike, intercepts Uke's right wrist with the left hand, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho).
3.Tori twists Uke's right wrist outward (Kote-gaeshi), brings Uke down flat on the back, and disarms the knife safely.
Kodokan Key Point: Kote-gaeshi disarm — maintain two-handed control of Uke's wrist to strip the knife cleanly after Uke hits the tatami.
Knife (Tantō)Overhead Knife StabKote-gaeshi Disarm
16
振上 Furiage "Raising the Stick"

Uke holds the stick with both hands and raises it overhead to strike. Tori steps deep inside before the stick can descend, drives a fist into Uke's solar plexus, and throws Uke with a hip throw (O-goshi / Uki-goshi).

1.Uke raises the wooden staff (Jō) high overhead with both hands, preparing to strike downward.
2.Tori steps in swiftly with the right foot inside Uke's weapon arc, striking Uke's solar plexus (Suigetsu) with a right fist.
3.Tori slides the right arm around Uke's waist, turns the hips, and throws Uke with a hip throw, seizing the stick upon completion.
Kodokan Key Point: Entering at inception — enter before the stick begins its downward descent to take away Uke's leverage.
Stick (Jō)Preemptive EntryHip Throw Disarm
17
振下 Furioroshi "Downward Stick Blow"

Uke swings the stick downward at Tori's head. Tori steps diagonally forward-left off the path of the stick, parries Uke's hands, delivers a palm strike to Uke's chin, and trips Uke with Osoto-gari.

1.Uke steps forward delivering a forceful two-handed downward strike with the stick aimed at Tori's head.
2.Tori steps forward-left (Tai-sabaki), letting the stick swing through empty space, and controls Uke's right forearm with the left hand.
3.Tori strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho), steps behind Uke's right leg, and executes Osoto-gari to throw Uke and disarm the stick.
Kodokan Key Point: Diagonal evasion & Osoto-gari — step outside the downward stick path while capturing Uke's lead arm.
Stick (Jō)Downward Strike EvasionOsoto-gari Disarm
18
両手突 Morote-tsuki "Two-Handed Stick Thrust"

Uke lunges forward with a two-handed spearing thrust of the stick toward Tori's stomach. Tori pivots to the left, grabs the stick with both hands, levers it against Uke's wrists, and trips Uke backward.

1.Uke steps forward thrusting the tip of the stick straight at Tori's solar plexus with both hands.
2.Tori steps the left foot back-left, turning sideways to let the stick pass, and grasps the end of the stick with both hands.
3.Tori twists and pushes the stick upward and backward, locking Uke's wrists, and throws Uke over backward (Maki-komi / stick lever).
Kodokan Key Point: Weapon leverage — turn Uke's own stick against their wrists to break balance and strip the weapon.
Stick (Jō)Spear Thrust EvasionStaff Lever Disarm
19
正面付 Shomen-zuke "Pistol Held to Abdomen (Front)"

Uke approaches from the front and points a pistol directly into Tori's abdomen, ordering Tori to raise hands. Tori raises hands, suddenly twists the hips to clear the line of fire, strips the gun inward, and points it at Uke.

1.Uke points a pistol directly at Tori's abdomen at close range, commanding Tori to raise hands.
2.Tori raises both hands to chest level in surrender, then suddenly pivots the hips to the left, pushing the gun barrel to the right with the right hand to clear the line of fire.
3.Tori's left hand grasps the gun barrel, twisting it inward over Uke's right index finger to wrench the weapon free, stepping back and covering Uke.
Kodokan Key Point: Clearing the line of fire — move the body off-line before wrenching the firearm inward against Uke's trigger finger.
Pistol (Kenjū)Point-Blank FrontFinger Lever Disarm
20
腰構 Koshi-gamae "Pistol Held at Hip Level"

Uke stands slightly back with the pistol held close to the right hip, demanding Tori surrender. Tori steps forward with hands raised, suddenly pivots inside, pins Uke's gun hand, strikes Uke's chin, and disarms the weapon.

1.Uke holds the pistol close to the right hip (out of Tori's reach) and orders Tori to surrender.
2.Tori feigns compliance, raises hands, steps forward with the right foot, and clamps Uke's gun hand to Uke's hip with the left hand while turning off the line of fire.
3.Tori strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho), twists the gun barrel outward to wrench it from Uke's grasp, and covers Uke.
Kodokan Key Point: Feigned surrender & closing distance — step inside to clamp the gun against Uke's hip before the trigger can be pulled.
Pistol (Kenjū)Hip Level PistolChin Strike & Disarm
21
背面付 Haimen-zuke "Pistol Held to the Back"

The final technique of Kodokan Goshin-jutsu. Uke presses the pistol directly into Tori's back. Tori raises hands, spins 180 degrees clockwise, knocks the gun away with the right upper arm, locks Uke's arm, and strips the firearm.

1.Uke presses the pistol barrel firmly into the center of Tori's back, demanding Tori surrender.
2.Tori raises both hands, then swiftly pivots 180 degrees clockwise on the left foot, knocking Uke's gun arm outward with the right upper arm/elbow.
3.Tori traps Uke's right arm under the right armpit, clamps the gun hand with both hands, twists the firearm out of Uke's grip, steps back, and aims the pistol at Uke to conclude the kata.
Kodokan Key Point: Explosive pivot — rotate the body 180 degrees smoothly on the ball of the foot to clear the muzzle trajectory in a fraction of a second.

Principles & Etiquette

Kodokan Reihō & Weapon Handling

Kodokan Goshin-jutsu requires precise martial etiquette, spatial awareness, and correct weapon handling protocols.

Weapon Etiquette (Buki no Reihō)

Before the armed division, Uke steps to the boundary, bows, inspects, and retrieves the Tantō, Jō, and Kenjū. After each disarm, Tori secures the weapon, steps back, and places it neatly for Uke to retrieve.

Atemi-waza Precision

All atemi strikes (fists, palm heels, elbows, stomps, and front kicks) must stop cleanly 2–3 cm before full anatomical contact with Uke's vital points (Kyusho) to ensure absolute safety.

Opening & Closing Reihō

Partners stand 5.5m apart. They perform standing bow (Ritsurei) to Joseki, face each other, drop to Seiza for seated bow (Zarei), rise, and step forward with the left foot into Shizentai.
