Introduction

What is Katame-waza Ura-no-Kata?

Katame-waza Ura-no-Kata (固技裏の形), often referred to as Katame-ura, is the tactical reverse counterpart (Ura) to the Kodokan Katame-no-Kata. Formalized and published in June 1970 by Kazuo Itō (9th Dan), it synthesizes the counter-groundwork principles inherited from his master, 10th Dan legend Kyūzō Mifune, alongside Kōsen newaza innovations from Jōin Oda (9th Dan).

While the standard Katame-no-Kata demonstrates Tori successfully applying 15 controls, the Ura-no-Kata explores the defender's perspective: as Tori attempts each classic technique, Uke anticipates the structural weakness, utilizes body shifts (Tai-sabaki), relieves leverage, and transitions directly into a decisive counter-pin, counter-choke, or counter-lock.

The kata is composed of 15 definitive reversal techniques organized into three symmetrical sets of five:
1. Osaekomi-waza Reversals (1–5): Counter-pins and armlocks against holding techniques (Kesa, Kata, Kami-shiho, Yoko-shiho, Kuzure-kami-shiho).
2. Shime-waza Reversals (6–10): Entangled armlocks, drop throws, and lever locks countering strangulations (Kata-juji, Hadaka, Okuri-eri, Kata-ha, Gyaku-juji).
3. Kansetsu-waza Reversals (11–15): Counter-locks, turnover pins, and leg entanglement reversals against armbars and locks (Ude-garami, Juji-gatame, Ude-gatame, Hiza-gatame, Ashi-garami).

Structure

The Three Counter Sets (15 Techniques)

Mirroring the 15 standard groundwork forms, each sequence demonstrates a fluid technical reversal into a submission or pin.

SET 01 — PIN REVERSALS

Osaekomi-waza Ura

抑込技の裏 — Holding Reversals

Turning scarf holds, shoulder pins, and four-corner immobilizations into counter-pins, neck compression, and joint locks.

  • 1. Kesa-gatame → Ude-tori Kuzure-kesa
  • 2. Kata-gatame → Gaeshi Hon-kesa-gatame
  • 3. Kami-shiho-gatame → Hasami-jime
  • 4. Yoko-shiho-gatame → Ude-hishigi-ude-gatame
  • 5. Kuzure-kami-shiho → Ude-hishigi-waki-gatame
SET 02 — CHOKE DEFENSES

Shime-waza Ura

絞技の裏 — Strangulation Reversals

Neutralizing collar chokes, naked strangles, and single-wing locks with keylocks, shoulder drops, and lever locks.

  • 6. Kata-juji-jime → Ude-garami
  • 7. Hadaka-jime → Seoi-otoshi / Juji-gatame
  • 8. Okuri-eri-jime → Ude-hishigi-teko-gatame
  • 9. Kata-ha-jime → Ude-tori Ushiro-kesa
  • 10. Gyaku-juji-jime → Ude-garami
SET 03 — JOINT-LOCK COUNTERS

Kansetsu-waza Ura

関節技の裏 — Armlock Reversals

Reversing keylocks, cross armlocks, and leg locks with counter-locks, forward push pins, and vertical chest pins.

  • 11. Ude-garami → Ude-garami (Counter)
  • 12. Ude-hishigi-juji → Kaeri-oki Osaekomi
  • 13. Ude-hishigi-ude → Oshi-taoshi Kesa-gatame
  • 14. Ude-hishigi-hiza → Ude-hishigi-ude-gatame
  • 15. Ashi-garami → Tate-shiho-gatame

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

Detailed breakdown of attack initiation by Tori and execution of the formal reversal (Kaeshi-waza) by Uke according to Kazuo Itō's syllabus.

01
袈裟固 → 腕取崩袈裟固 Kesa-gatame → Ude-tori Kuzure-kesa-gatame "Scarf Hold countered by Arm-Entangled Modified Scarf Hold"

As Tori settles into Hon-kesa-gatame, Uke joins hands, bridges explosively over the left shoulder to upend Tori's base, traps Tori's reaching right arm, and pins Tori in Kuzure-kesa-gatame.

1.Tori secures Hon-kesa-gatame, encircling Uke's neck with the right arm and controlling the sleeve.
2.Uke clasps both hands around Tori's torso, pulls Tori's weight directly over Uke's chest, and bridges hard over the shoulder.
3.As Tori rolls over onto the back, Uke maintains tight control of Tori's right arm and establishes Ude-tori Kuzure-kesa-gatame.
Itō Key Point: Align your hips directly beneath Tori's center of mass before initiating the bridge to eliminate dead weight.

02
肩固 → 返本袈裟固 Kata-gatame → Gaeshi Hon-kesa-gatame "Shoulder Hold countered by Reversed Standard Scarf Hold"

As Tori locks the head and arm in Kata-gatame, Uke hooks Tori's trailing leg, bridges forcefully toward Tori's head angle, rolls Tori over, and transitions into Hon-kesa-gatame.

1.Tori traps Uke's right arm across the neck and establishes Kata-gatame on the right side.
2.Uke joins hands to relieve carotid pressure, hooks Tori's right leg with the left foot, and bridges diagonally.
3.Tori's base collapses; Uke turns through the roll and locks Tori in Hon-kesa-gatame.
Itō Key Point: Trap the trailing post leg immediately to prevent Tori from widening the base to absorb the reversal bridge.

03
上四方固 → 挟絞 Kami-shiho-gatame → Hasami-jime "Upper Four-Corner Hold countered by Scissors Strangle"

Tori pins Uke from the top in Kami-shiho-gatame. Uke grips Tori's belt on both sides, bridges upward to create space, wraps both legs around Tori's neck, and applies the Hasami-jime leg scissors strangle.

1.Tori secures Kami-shiho-gatame, gripping Uke's belt on both sides over the shoulders.
2.Uke grips Tori's belt, bridges high onto one shoulder to displace Tori's chest, and swings both legs up around Tori's neck.
3.Uke crosses the ankles and extends the hips, applying a decisive Hasami-jime strangle.
Itō Key Point: Use the pendulum momentum of the legs during the bridge to whip the legs around Tori's neck before posture resets.

04
横四方固 → 腕挫腕固 Yoko-shiho-gatame → Ude-hishigi-ude-gatame "Side Four-Corner Hold countered by Arm Entanglement Armlock"

Tori attempts Yoko-shiho-gatame across Uke's chest. Uke creates a frame against the neck, traps Tori's reaching left arm across the chest, rotates the hips, and locks the elbow in Ude-gatame.

1.Tori moves perpendicular across Uke's chest to set Yoko-shiho-gatame, reaching between Uke's legs.
2.Uke frames the forearm against Tori's jaw, shrimps the hips away, and captures Tori's neck-controlling arm.
3.Uke clamps both hands over Tori's extended elbow, hyperextending the joint with Ude-gatame to force submission.
Itō Key Point: Frame before chest-to-chest contact is locked down, using the space created to isolate the reaching arm.

05
崩上四方固 → 腕挫腋固 Kuzure-kami-shiho-gatame → Ude-hishigi-waki-gatame "Modified Upper Four-Corner Hold countered by Armpit Armlock"

Tori attacks with Kuzure-kami-shiho with an arm underhooked. Uke traps the underhooking arm against the torso, bridges and turns outward, clamping the arm in Waki-gatame.

1.Tori applies Kuzure-kami-shiho-gatame, underhooking Uke's right shoulder and gripping the collar.
2.Uke clamps down on Tori's underhooking arm, pinching it tightly beneath the armpit.
3.Uke pivots the hips outward and applies downward chest and armpit leverage, locking the elbow in Waki-gatame.
Itō Key Point: Trap the reaching arm immediately against the ribs so Tori cannot withdraw or post the limb.

06
片十字絞 → 腕緘 Kata-juji-jime → Ude-garami "Half Cross Strangle countered by Entangled Armlock"

Tori attempts Kata-juji-jime from the mount. Uke pushes upward on Tori's crossing elbow to relieve the choke, captures the upper arm in a figure-four grip, bridges, and executes Ude-garami.

1.Tori crosses the hands on Uke's collar from mount to apply Kata-juji-jime.
2.Uke relieves airway pressure by pushing Tori's top elbow across the centerline, weaving the arms into a figure-four grip.
3.Uke bridges to reverse position and rotates Tori's shoulder into a finishing Ude-garami lock.
Itō Key Point: Open the trachea immediately by pushing the crossing elbow across before locking the figure-four grip.

07
裸絞 → 背負落 / 腕挫十字固 Hadaka-jime → Seoi-otoshi / Ude-hishigi-juji-gatame "Naked Strangle countered by Shoulder Drop / Cross Armlock"

Tori applies Hadaka-jime from the rear. Uke pulls down on Tori's choking forearm with both hands, ducks beneath the arm to drop Tori forward with Seoi-otoshi, and transitions directly into Juji-gatame.

1.Tori locks the right forearm across Uke's throat in Hadaka-jime from behind.
2.Uke immediately grasps Tori's choking wrist with both hands, pulls downward, tucks the chin, and turns the hips.
3.Uke drops forward in Seoi-otoshi, dumping Tori over the shoulder, and extends Tori's arm for Juji-gatame.
Itō Key Point: Secure two-on-one downward traction on the choking forearm before initiating the shoulder turn.

08
送襟絞 → 腕挫梃子固 Okuri-eri-jime → Ude-hishigi-teko-gatame "Sliding Lapel Strangle countered by Lever Armlock"

Tori applies Okuri-eri-jime from the rear. Uke controls Tori's choking hand, turns 180 degrees inward toward Tori's chest, traps the choking arm across the shoulder, and levers the elbow in Teko-gatame.

1.Tori feeds the collar around Uke's neck in Okuri-eri-jime.
2.Uke grips Tori's primary choking sleeve with both hands and spins 180 degrees inward to face Tori directly.
3.By turning chest-to-chest, Uke neutralizes the strangle and uses the shoulder as a fulcrum to lever Tori's elbow in Teko-gatame.
Itō Key Point: Turn towards the choking hand to unravel lapel leverage instantly, converting the attack into an armbar fulcrum.

09
片羽絞 → 腕取後袈裟固 Kata-ha-jime → Ude-tori Ushiro-kesa-gatame "Single-Wing Strangle countered by Arm-Entangled Rear Scarf Hold"

Tori lifts Uke's arm for Kata-ha-jime. Uke reaches back to trap Tori's threading arm, pivots the hips backward against Tori's base, and establishes Ushiro-kesa-gatame.

1.Tori reaches under Uke's left arm and behind the neck to apply Kata-ha-jime.
2.Uke clamps the left arm tightly to the ribs, trapping Tori's threading wrist.
3.Uke bridges backward into Tori, spins the hips, and consolidates control in Ude-tori Ushiro-kesa-gatame.
Itō Key Point: Trap the wing arm before Tori locks the hand behind your occiput.

10
逆十字絞 → 腕緘 Gyaku-juji-jime → Ude-garami "Reverse Cross Strangle countered by Entangled Armlock"

Tori attacks with palms-up Gyaku-juji-jime from the guard. Uke reaches inside, pushes the elbows outward to strip the choke leverage, traps one arm in a figure-four, and finishes with Ude-garami.

1.Tori grips with four fingers inside both collars in Gyaku-juji-jime.
2.Uke drives both forearms between Tori's crossed arms, wedging the wrists outward to break the grip.
3.Uke weaves the right arm around Tori's displaced left arm and secures an Ude-garami keylock.
Itō Key Point: Strip the crossed wrists outward against the thumbs before Tori's elbows flare to generate choke tension.

11
腕緘 → 腕緘（返） Ude-garami → Ude-garami (Counter) "Entangled Armlock countered by Reverse Entangled Armlock"

Tori attacks with Ude-garami. Uke extends the captured arm before the shoulder rotates, reaches over Tori's controlling arm, locks a reverse figure-four, and applies counter Ude-garami.

1.Tori grips Uke's wrist and weaves the arm for an Ude-garami lock.
2.Before the shoulder lock tightens, Uke straightens the arm and rotates the thumb outward.
3.Uke wraps the free arm over Tori's forearm, weaves a reverse figure-four lock, and submits Tori with counter Ude-garami.
Itō Key Point: Straighten the arm immediately — Ude-garami requires a 90-degree elbow bend to torque the shoulder.

12
腕挫十字固 → 返起抑込 Ude-hishigi-juji-gatame → Kaeri-oki Osaekomi "Cross Armlock countered by Stacked Return Pin"

Tori sits back for Juji-gatame. Uke clasps hands, stacks all bodyweight directly over Tori's neck and shoulders, slips the elbow to the mat, and establishes a dominant top pin (Kaeri-oki Osaekomi).

1.Tori swings the leg over Uke's head and sits back for Juji-gatame.
2.Uke clasps hands and drives forward, stacking all weight onto Tori's upper cervical spine.
3.Uke circles the head around Tori's leg, slips the trapped elbow to the floor, and pins Tori in Kaeri-oki Osaekomi.
Itō Key Point: Heavy forward stack — kill Tori's pelvic extension leverage by driving weight onto the shoulders.

13
腕挫腕固 → 押倒袈裟固 Ude-hishigi-ude-gatame → Oshi-taoshi Kesa-gatame "Arm Armlock countered by Push-Down Scarf Hold"

Tori applies straight armlock pressure with Ude-gatame. Uke bends the elbow inward to relieve the lock, drives forward across Tori's chest, pushes Tori flat, and pins in Kesa-gatame.

1.Tori traps Uke's straight arm against the shoulder in Ude-gatame.
2.Uke rotates the forearm inward, shifting the elbow fulcrum away from Tori's pressure.
3.Uke drives forward with body weight (Oshi-taoshi), flattening Tori and securing Kesa-gatame.
Itō Key Point: Radial bone rotation — changing the elbow's angle neutralizes straight armbar hyperextension.

14
腕挫膝固 → 腕挫腕固 Ude-hishigi-hiza-gatame → Ude-hishigi-ude-gatame "Knee Armlock countered by Arm Entanglement Armlock"

Tori applies Hiza-gatame using the knee as a fulcrum. Uke pushes Tori's knee off line with the free hand, steps past the leg, traps Tori's extended arm, and applies counter Ude-gatame.

1.Tori places the knee across Uke's elbow in Hiza-gatame from the guard.
2.Uke pushes Tori's knee downward across the centerline with the free hand to clear the joint.
3.Uke steps past the displaced leg, clamps both hands on Tori's outstretched arm, and applies Ude-gatame.
Itō Key Point: Deflect the knee fulcrum across the center before joint hyperextension is fully set.

15
足緘 → 縦四方固 Ashi-garami → Tate-shiho-gatame "Entangled Leglock countered by Vertical Four-Corner Hold"

Tori attempts the classical Ashi-garami leg entanglement. Uke unwinds the controlling foot, clears the knee line, steps completely over Tori's hips, and pins in Tate-shiho-gatame.

1.Tori wraps the legs around Uke's leg and hip in Ashi-garami.
2.Uke peels Tori's hooking foot off the hip and pulls the knee free of the entanglement.
3.Uke steps over Tori's pelvis, dropping chest-to-chest to secure Tate-shiho-gatame.
Itō Key Point: Clear the knee line — once the knee escapes the entanglement, all leglock leverage collapses completely.

Principles & Reversals

Tactical Theory of Ne-waza Reversals

Kazuo Itō's groundwork reversal pedagogy builds on three foundational pillars of classical grappling.

Kuzushi from the Ground

The defender under a pin or submission must create unbalancing (Kuzushi) using hip movement (Ebi) and explosive bridging to make the top player's base light and unstable.

Early Joint Alignment

Joint lock counters rely on rotating the radial bone and clearing fulcrums before the hyperextension lock is fully engaged, neutralizing the opponent's mechanical advantage.

Omote & Ura Harmony

Every offensive groundwork technique possesses a structural vulnerability, embodying the fundamental principle of Ju-yoku-go-o-seisu (gentleness overcomes hardness).
