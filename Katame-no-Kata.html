Introduction

What is Katame-no-Kata?

Katame-no-Kata (固の形), meaning "Forms of Grappling" or "Immobilization," is the second half of Kodokan Judo's Randori-no-Kata (alongside Nage-no-Kata). Developed between 1884 and 1887 under Jigoro Kano, it teaches the fundamental mechanics of control without reliance on raw muscular force.

While Nage-no-Kata focuses on dynamic standing throws, Katame-no-Kata focuses entirely on ground grappling (Ne-waza). The kata is arranged into three sets of five techniques: holding techniques (Osaekomi-waza), strangulations (Shime-waza), and joint locks (Kansetsu-waza).

In the holding section, Uke makes three energetic, realistic escape attempts (Kaisō). Tori must neutralize every escape attempt by shifting their center of gravity, adjusting hip angles, and altering weight distribution to keep Uke immobilized.

Movement on the mat is conducted in high-kneeling posture (Kyoshi) and knee-walking (Shikko), making Katame-no-Kata a rigorous masterclass in stability, breathing, and mutual safety.

Structure

The Three Sets

Katame-no-Kata is structured into three disciplines (Dan), each featuring five standard techniques representing the pillars of Kodokan groundwork.

SET 01

Osaekomi-waza

抑込技 — Holding Techniques

Controlling Uke supine on the tatami through chest-to-chest contact, hip pressure, and balance adjustments against 3 distinct escape attempts.

  • 1. Kesa-gatame Scarf Hold
  • 2. Kata-gatame Shoulder Hold
  • 3. Kami-shiho-gatame Upper Four-Quarter
  • 4. Yoko-shiho-gatame Side Four-Quarter
  • 5. Kuzure-kami-shiho Modified Upper
SET 02

Shime-waza

絞技 — Strangulation Techniques

Restricting blood flow via carotid compression using the gi lapels or forearm blades from mounted, rear, and guard positions.

  • 6. Kata-juji-jime Half Cross Strangle
  • 7. Hadaka-jime Naked Strangle
  • 8. Okuri-eri-jime Sliding Collar
  • 9. Kata-ha-jime Single Wing
  • 10. Gyaku-juji-jime Reverse Cross
SET 03

Kansetsu-waza

関節技 — Joint Locking Techniques

Hyperextending and twisting the elbow joint using fulcrums created by the arms, knees, legs, and pelvis.

  • 11. Ude-garami Arm Entanglement
  • 12. Ude-hishigi-juji-gatame Cross Armlock
  • 13. Ude-hishigi-ude-gatame Arm Armlock
  • 14. Ude-hishigi-hiza-gatame Knee Armlock
  • 15. Ude-hishigi-ashi-gatame Leg Armlock

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

Each technique is detailed according to the official Kodokan Judo textbook standard, including positioning, mechanics, and escape responses.

01
袈裟固 Kesa-gatame "Scarf Hold"

Tori sits with their right hip against Uke's right flank, wraps their left arm around Uke's neck, and locks Uke's right arm under their left armpit.

1.Tori approaches Uke's right side in Kyoshi, stepping the right foot forward and lowering the right hip beside Uke's chest.
2.Left hand encircles Uke's neck, gripping the rear collar; right hand firmly clamps Uke's right upper arm to Tori's ribs.
3.Tori extends the right leg forward and bends the left leg back, lowering the hips firmly onto the tatami.
Kodokan Escape Attempts (Kaisō): 1. Uke bridges into Tori attempting to roll them over; 2. Uke pushes Tori's chin with the left hand to free the right arm; 3. Uke bridges away to slip out.
Osaekomi-wazaSide ControlWide Base
02
肩固 Kata-gatame "Shoulder Hold"

Tori pushes Uke's right arm across Uke's neck, clasps hands around Uke's neck and arm, and pins downward with the right knee against Uke's ribs.

1.Tori shifts from Kesa-gatame, pushing Uke's right upper arm across Uke's throat.
2.Tori slides the right arm behind Uke's neck and clasps hands firmly at the side of Uke's head.
3.Tori plants the right knee against Uke's ribs, extends the left leg wide, and drives chest pressure down.
Kodokan Escape Attempts (Kaisō): 1. Uke clasps hands to pull the trapped arm back; 2. Uke turns body inward into Tori; 3. Uke bridges diagonally to the right.
Osaekomi-wazaShoulder ClampChest Pressure
03
上四方固 Kami-shiho-gatame "Upper Four-Quarter Hold"

From above Uke's head, Tori slides both arms under Uke's shoulders to grip the belt on both sides, lowering the chest firmly onto Uke's chest.

1.From Kyoshi at Uke's head, Tori slides both arms under Uke's shoulders to grip the belt.
2.Tori lowers their chest onto Uke's chest and rests their forehead against Uke's lower abdomen.
3.Tori spreads both knees wide with toes engaged in Kiza, maintaining a low, balanced center of mass.
Kodokan Escape Attempts (Kaisō): 1. Uke bridges and turns hips to roll Tori left; 2. Uke bridges to roll Tori right; 3. Uke pushes Tori's shoulders to slide out.
Osaekomi-wazaHead-On PinBelt Grips
04
横四方固 Yoko-shiho-gatame "Side Four-Quarter Hold"

Tori positions perpendicularly across Uke's right flank, threading the left arm under Uke's neck and the right arm between Uke's legs to grip the belt.

1.Tori approaches Uke's right flank, sliding the left arm under Uke's neck to grip the back collar.
2.Tori passes the right arm between Uke's thighs to hold the rear belt.
3.Tori drops chest weight onto Uke's chest, spreading knees wide with hips low.
Kodokan Escape Attempts (Kaisō): 1. Uke inserts the left knee between bodies; 2. Uke wraps the right arm around Tori's neck to roll; 3. Uke pushes Tori's head to turn face down.
Osaekomi-wazaPerpendicularCrotch Grip
05
崩上四方固 Kuzure-kami-shiho-gatame "Modified Upper Four-Quarter Hold"

From Uke's head, Tori wraps the right arm over Uke's right shoulder/collar and passes the left arm under Uke's left armpit to grip the belt.

1.From Uke's head, Tori wraps the right arm over Uke's right shoulder to hold the collar.
2.Tori threads the left arm under Uke's left armpit to grip the back belt.
3.Tori presses their torso down diagonally across Uke's chest, controlling Uke's head.
Kodokan Escape Attempts (Kaisō): 1. Uke bridges and turns right to free the shoulder; 2. Uke turns left to extract head; 3. Uke bridges straight up.
Osaekomi-wazaAsymmetricalHead Immobilization
06
片十字絞 Kata-juji-jime "Half Cross Strangle"

Tori sits mounted astride Uke's hips (Tate-shiho), gripping one collar palm up and the other palm down to cross the wrists and constrict the carotids.

1.Tori mounts Uke in Tate-shiho position.
2.Left hand grips deep into Uke's left collar with four fingers inside (palm up).
3.Right hand crosses over, inserting the thumb inside Uke's right collar (palm down), drawing elbows in and pulling chest close.
Submission Protocol: Uke signals submission by tapping twice (Mairi) on Tori or the tatami. Tori releases immediately.
Shime-wazaMountedCross Grip
07
裸絞 Hadaka-jime "Naked Strangle"

Applied from behind a seated Uke without using the gi. The radial edge of Tori's right forearm wraps around the throat while Tori clasps palms together.

1.Uke sits in Seiza; Tori kneels behind in Kyoshi.
2.Tori wraps the radial blade of the right forearm across Uke's throat.
3.Tori clasps palms at Uke's left shoulder, presses head against Uke's occiput, and expands chest.
Submission Protocol: Uke signals submission by tapping twice on Tori's thigh.
Shime-wazaRear PositionNo-Gi Choke
08
送襟絞 Okuri-eri-jime "Sliding Collar Strangle"

From behind the seated Uke, Tori reaches around the neck to take a deep collar grip while the other hand pulls down on the opposite lapel under the armpit.

1.Tori reaches right hand over Uke's right shoulder, gripping deep into Uke's left collar (thumb inside).
2.Left hand passes under Uke's left armpit to grip the right lapel, pulling down to tighten the jacket across the neck.
3.Tori draws Uke back against their chest, sliding the collar to constrict the carotid arteries.
Submission Protocol: Uke taps twice to submit.
Shime-wazaRear PositionSliding Collar
09
片羽絞 Kata-ha-jime "Single Wing Strangle"

Tori sets a deep right collar grip from behind, threads the left arm under Uke's left armpit, and places the left palm behind Uke's neck.

1.Tori secures a deep right collar grip around Uke's left neck as in Okuri-eri-jime.
2.Tori raises Uke's left arm, threads the left arm under the armpit, and anchors the palm behind Uke's neck.
3.Tori lifts Uke's trapped arm (the "wing") while tightening the collar with the right hand.
Submission Protocol: Uke taps twice to submit.
Shime-wazaWing LockShoulder Control
10
逆十字絞 Gyaku-juji-jime "Reverse Cross Strangle"

Mounted atop Uke, Tori crosses forearms and inserts both thumbs deep inside both collars with four fingers outside (both palms facing up toward Tori).

1.Tori sits mounted astride Uke's hips (Tate-shiho).
2.Tori crosses forearms and inserts both thumbs deep into both collars with palms facing up.
3.Tori lowers the torso, straightens the wrists, and expands the chest to complete the choke.
Submission Protocol: Uke taps twice to submit.
Shime-wazaMountedDouble Palms Up
11
腕緘 Ude-garami "Arm Entanglement / Keylock"

Tori pins Uke's bent right arm to the mat (elbow at 90 degrees), weaves their right arm under Uke's upper arm, and grasps their own left wrist to form a figure-four lock.

1.Tori pins Uke's right wrist to the mat with the left hand.
2.Tori threads the right arm under Uke's right triceps and grabs their own left wrist.
3.Tori lifts Uke's elbow while sliding the wrist along the mat toward Uke's hips.
Submission Protocol: Uke taps twice immediately due to joint rotational torque.
Kansetsu-wazaFigure-FourShoulder & Elbow Lock
12
腕挫十字固 Ude-hishigi-juji-gatame "Cross Armlock"

Tori traps Uke's extended right arm between the thighs, places one leg over Uke's neck and one over the chest, and extends the hips to hyperextend the elbow.

1.Tori secures Uke's right arm with both hands, placing left leg over Uke's neck and right leg across the chest.
2.Tori clamps knees tightly together, keeping Uke's thumb pointing straight toward the ceiling.
3.Tori leans back smoothly, elevating the hips to hyperextend the elbow across the pelvis.
Submission Protocol: Uke taps twice with the free hand or foot.
Kansetsu-wazaCross BodyPelvic Fulcrum
13
腕挫腕固 Ude-hishigi-ude-gatame "Arm Armlock"

As Uke pushes up, Tori traps Uke's wrist against their own neck/shoulder, placing both hands on Uke's elbow joint to apply downward leverage.

1.Tori traps Uke's right wrist tightly against the side of their neck and shoulder.
2.Tori places both hands directly over Uke's extended elbow joint (palms layered).
3.Tori twists the torso slightly and applies downward, leveraged pressure against the joint.
Submission Protocol: Uke taps twice on the tatami.
Kansetsu-wazaDirect Arm LockNeck Trap
14
腕挫膝固 Ude-hishigi-hiza-gatame "Knee Armlock"

Tori traps Uke's extended right arm under the left armpit and uses the right knee directly against the elbow joint as a fulcrum to apply hyperextension.

1.Tori secures Uke's right wrist under the left armpit.
2.Tori positions the right knee/shin directly on the outside of Uke's right elbow joint.
3.Tori pushes the knee forward while pulling Uke's upper torso inward, hyperextending the elbow.
Submission Protocol: Uke taps twice.
Kansetsu-wazaKnee FulcrumLeverage
15
腕挫脚固 Ude-hishigi-ashi-gatame "Leg Armlock"

The final technique of Katame-no-Kata. Tori captures Uke's extended arm, drapes their leg/calf over the elbow joint, and uses bodyweight leverage to apply hyperextension.

1.Tori traps Uke's extended arm flat against the mat.
2.Tori drapes their leg over Uke's triceps/elbow, hooking the foot underneath to anchor the fulcrum.
3.Tori applies controlled downward pressure through the calf and bodyweight to hyperextend the joint.
Submission Protocol: Uke taps twice to conclude the 15th technique of Katame-no-Kata.
Kansetsu-wazaLeg FulcrumFinal Technique

Principles & Etiquette

Kodokan Reihō & Floor Protocol

Kata begins and ends with formal respect (Rei). Smooth execution depends on strictly adhering to Kodokan mat rules.

Opening & Closing Distance

Tori and Uke stand approximately 5.5 meters apart (6 tatami lengths). Tori stands to the right of Joseki, Uke to the left. Both perform Ritsurei toward Joseki, turn to face each other, drop to Seiza, and execute Zarei.

Kyoshi & Shikko Movement

All floor transitions in Katame-no-Kata are conducted from Kyoshi (high kneeling on one knee with toes in Kiza). Movements across the tatami are performed with smooth knee-walking (Shikko).

Mairi (Submission) Signal

Uke signals submission by tapping twice (Mairi) with the free hand or foot on Tori or the mat. Tori must release all pressure immediately and smoothly return to Kyoshi.
