Introduction

What is Kime-no-Kata?

Kime-no-Kata (極の形), also historically termed Shinken Shobu no Kata (Forms of Real Combat), was developed around 1888 under Jigoro Kano with senior jujutsu and kenjutsu masters. It preserves the lethal, combat-tested self-defense roots of traditional jujutsu within Kodokan Judo.

The kata is composed of 20 techniques partitioned into two distinct physical domains: Idori (居捕 — Kneeling/Seated techniques, 8 techniques) and Tachiai (立合 — Standing techniques, 12 techniques).

Within both divisions, Tori defends against both unarmed attacks (strikes, grabs, chokes, and hair seizures) and traditional samurai weaponry: the Tantō (短刀 — dagger) and the Katana (刀 — long sword).

Unlike sportive kata, Kime-no-Kata demands immediate decisiveness (Kime), uncompromising speed, devastating counter-strikes (Atemi-waza) aimed at vital points (Kyūsho), and immediate joint dislocations or neck breaks to permanently neutralize real-world lethal threats.

Structure

The Four Technical Groups (20 Techniques)

Kime-no-Kata is categorized first by posture (Idori vs. Tachiai), and second by whether the assailant is unarmed (Toshu) or wielding a blade (Buki).

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

All 20 techniques detailed step-by-step according to the official Kodokan Judo textbook standard.

01
両手取 Ryote-dori "Both Wrists Seized (Kneeling)"

Seated in Seiza facing each other, Uke seizes both of Tori's wrists to pin them. Tori raises the wrists outward to break balance, drives a double-fist strike (Ryote-tsuki) into Uke's solar plexus, ducks under Uke's arm, and forces Uke down with a kneeling shoulder armlock.

1.Both kneel in Seiza at close distance (approx. 1 tatami length). Uke reaches forward and grips both of Tori's wrists with thumbs underneath.
2.Tori rises onto the toes in Kiza, opens both wrists outward in a circle to unbalance Uke forward, and drives the right fist into Uke's solar plexus (Suigetsu).
3.Tori steps the left knee forward, twists Uke's right arm outward into an armlock (Ude-hishigi-waki-gatame variation), pinning Uke flat onto the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Transition to Kiza — immediately flex the toes into Kiza upon the grab to establish drive and striking power.

02
突掛 Tsukkake "Punch to Face (Kneeling)"

Uke rises to Kiza and throws a straight right punch at Tori's face. Tori leans back to avoid the blow, parries Uke's right elbow with the left palm, strikes Uke's armpit/solar plexus with the right fist, and forces Uke to the tatami in an armlock.

1.Uke rises into Kiza and launches a direct right fist thrust at Tori's face.
2.Tori assumes Kiza, dodges backward slightly, blocks Uke's right elbow with the left palm, and drives the right fist into Uke's right armpit/ribs.
3.Tori secures Uke's right wrist with the right hand, places the left forearm over Uke's elbow, and forces Uke face-down into an armlock (Ude-gatame).
Kodokan Key Point: Elbow parry & counter-strike — deflect Uke's punch at the elbow while delivering the simultaneous fist strike.
IdoriUnarmedPunch EvasionUde-gatame
03
擦上 Suri-age "Forehead Thrust / Palm Strike (Kneeling)"

Uke rises and drives an upward palm-heel strike toward Tori's forehead. Tori parries with both hands upward, steps forward-right to slip under Uke's arm, drives a fist to Uke's solar plexus, and pins Uke with a twisting armlock.

1.Uke rises in Kiza and thrusts the open right palm upward aimed directly between Tori's eyebrows (Uto).
2.Tori raises both hands to deflect Uke's rising forearm upward, stepping the right knee forward inside Uke's space.
3.Tori delivers a right fist to Uke's solar plexus, wraps the right arm around Uke's right elbow, and levers Uke down prone onto the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Upward deflection — sweep both hands up to guide Uke's thrust overhead before entering the knee.
IdoriUnarmedPalm ParryInside Control
04
横打 Yoko-uchi "Temple Strike (Kneeling)"

Uke delivers a diagonal downward hammer-fist blow toward Tori's left temple. Tori ducks inside, blocks Uke's right wrist with the left hand, strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho), and throws Uke backward onto the mat.

1.Uke raises the right fist in Kiza and executes a diagonal downward chopping blow aimed at Tori's left temple.
2.Tori slides the right knee forward-left inside the blow, blocks Uke's right forearm with the left forearm, and drives a right palm heel (Teisho) into Uke's chin.
3.Tori snaps Uke's head back, grabs Uke's right shoulder, and drives Uke straight back flat onto the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Teisho to the chin — drive the base of the palm upward into Uke's jaw to break the spine's alignment.
IdoriUnarmedTemple BlockChin Strike
05
後取 Ushiro-dori "Rear Seizure (Kneeling)"

Uke approaches from behind a kneeling Tori and grabs both of Tori's shoulders to pin them. Tori drops the hips, strikes Uke's solar plexus with a rear elbow, wraps Uke's right arm, and pulls Uke over the shoulder in a kneeling throw.

1.Uke approaches silently from behind and seizes both of Tori's shoulders from the rear.
2.Tori flexes into Kiza, slides the hips back against Uke, and drives a sharp left elbow strike (Ushiro-ate) into Uke's ribs/solar plexus.
3.Tori seizes Uke's right wrist, pulls it over the right shoulder, pivots, and throws Uke forward over the shoulder to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Rear elbow atemi — deliver the backward elbow strike before Uke can establish a choking hold.
IdoriUnarmedRear AttackSeated Shoulder Throw
06
突込 Tsukikomi "Direct Dagger Thrust (Kneeling)"

Uke draws a dagger (Tantō) and thrusts straight at Tori's abdomen while kneeling. Tori pivots off the line, parries Uke's wrist, locks the elbow into Waki-gatame, forces Uke to the mat, and disarms the blade.

1.Uke draws the dagger from the belt and thrusts directly at Tori's abdomen in Kiza.
2.Tori shifts the hips back-left off the blade's line, parries Uke's right wrist with the left hand, and strikes Uke's eyes/face with the right open hand.
3.Tori wraps the right arm over Uke's right elbow in Waki-gatame, drives Uke face-down, and strips the dagger from Uke's hand.
Kodokan Key Point: Hips off the blade path — turn the torso completely away from the dagger's line before locking the arm.

07
切込 Kiri-komi "Downward Dagger Cut (Kneeling)"

Uke raises the dagger overhead and delivers a downward ice-pick slash at Tori's crown. Tori steps inside with the right knee, blocks Uke's wrist with the left hand, drives a palm heel to Uke's chin, and applies Kote-gaeshi to disarm.

1.Uke raises the dagger overhead in reverse grip and executes a downward stab at Tori's head.
2.Tori slides the right knee forward inside the attack, blocks Uke's right wrist with the left hand, and drives a right palm heel (Teisho) into Uke's chin.
3.Tori grasps Uke's right hand with both hands, twists the wrist outward in Kote-gaeshi, throws Uke backward, and secures the blade.
Kodokan Key Point: Kneeling Kote-gaeshi — maintain full downward torque on the wrist until Uke is flat on the back.
IdoriDagger (Tantō)Downward StabKote-gaeshi
08
横突 Yoko-tsuki "Side Dagger Thrust (Kneeling)"

Seated side-by-side, Uke suddenly draws the dagger and thrusts sideways into Tori's ribs. Tori steps the left knee back, parries the wrist, locks Uke's elbow across the chest, and forces Uke to submit.

1.Sitting side-by-side in Seiza, Uke draws the dagger and thrusts laterally toward Tori's right flank.
2.Tori pivots to face Uke, catches Uke's right wrist with the right hand, and drives the left elbow/palm into Uke's chin.
3.Tori applies an armlock over Uke's extended elbow (Ude-hishigi-ude-gatame), forces Uke down, and strips the knife.
Kodokan Key Point: Lateral pivot — quickly turn the entire body toward Uke to intercept the side thrust.

09
両手取 Ryote-dori "Both Wrists Seized (Standing)"

Standing facing each other, Uke seizes both of Tori's wrists. Tori raises both arms outward to unbalance Uke, delivers a front kick (Mae-geri) to Uke's groin, and applies an outward wrist lock (Kote-gaeshi) to pin Uke.

1.Both stand in Shizentai. Uke advances and firmly seizes both of Tori's wrists.
2.Tori rotates both wrists outward while delivering a right front snap kick (Mae-geri) to Uke's testicles (Kinteki).
3.Tori steps back with the left foot, twists Uke's right wrist into Kote-gaeshi, throws Uke to the mat, and locks the arm for submission.
Kodokan Key Point: Simultaneous kick & wrist rotation — kick the groin at the exact moment the wrists rotate outward.
TachiaiUnarmedGroin KickKote-gaeshi
10
袖取 Sode-tori "Sleeve Seized from Side"

Walking side-by-side, Uke grabs Tori's left sleeve from the side and attempts to pull. Tori turns, delivers a right palm-heel strike to Uke's chin, hooks Uke's leg with Osoto-gari, and throws Uke flat to the mat.

1.Walking forward side-by-side, Uke grabs Tori's left sleeve at the elbow with the right hand.
2.Tori steps the right foot forward, pivots left, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho).
3.Tori steps deep behind Uke's right leg and executes a decisive Osoto-gari to take Uke down.
Kodokan Key Point: Pivot & Osoto-gari — turn into Uke's pull immediately and drive the heel strike into the chin while reaping the leg.
TachiaiUnarmedSleeve GrabOsoto-gari
11
突掛 Tsukkake "Straight Face Punch (Standing)"

Uke steps in with a straight right punch to Tori's face. Tori steps back-left, parries the blow at the elbow with the left hand, drives a right fist to Uke's solar plexus, and levers Uke down with Waki-gatame.

1.Uke steps forward with the right foot, throwing a straight right punch at Tori's face.
2.Tori steps back-left with the left foot, parries Uke's right elbow with the left palm, and punches Uke's solar plexus with the right fist.
3.Tori wraps the right arm over Uke's right elbow, applies Waki-gatame, and pins Uke face-down onto the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Linear parry & Waki-gatame — parry the incoming punch and trap the extended elbow in one seamless motion.
TachiaiUnarmedPunch DefenseWaki-gatame
12
突上 Tsuki-age "Uppercut to Chin (Standing)"

Uke steps in with a powerful rising right uppercut to Tori's chin. Tori tilts the head back, intercepts Uke's wrist with the left hand, drives a right palm strike to Uke's chin, and throws Uke with Kote-gaeshi.

1.Uke advances with the right foot, launching a rising uppercut aimed directly at Tori's jaw.
2.Tori steps back with the left foot to clear the jawline, intercepts Uke's right wrist with the left hand, and strikes Uke's chin with the right palm heel.
3.Tori grasps Uke's wrist with both hands, twists it outward in Kote-gaeshi, and brings Uke down flat on the back.
Kodokan Key Point: Head clearance — tilt the chin back just enough to avoid the fist while securing the wrist interception.
TachiaiUnarmedUppercut DefenseKote-gaeshi
13
擦上 Suri-age "Forehead Strike (Standing)"

Uke delivers an upward palm-heel thrust toward Tori's forehead. Tori parries with both hands upward, steps forward-right inside Uke's guard, punches the solar plexus, and throws Uke backward.

1.Uke steps forward thrusting the right palm-heel upward aimed between Tori's eyes.
2.Tori deflects Uke's forearm upward with both open palms, stepping the right foot forward inside Uke's stance.
3.Tori punches Uke's solar plexus with the right fist, wraps the right arm around Uke's neck/torso, and throws Uke backward onto the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Inside entry — enter deep into Uke's center while deflecting the palm thrust upward.
TachiaiUnarmedPalm DeflectionInside Throw
14
横打 Yoko-uchi "Diagonal Temple Chop (Standing)"

Uke delivers a diagonal hammer-fist blow to Tori's left temple. Tori ducks inside, blocks Uke's forearm, drives a palm strike into Uke's chin, steps behind Uke's right leg, and executes Osoto-gari.

1.Uke advances with the right foot, striking diagonally downward with a hammer fist toward Tori's left temple.
2.Tori steps forward-left inside the blow, blocks Uke's forearm with the left hand, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho).
3.Tori steps deep behind Uke's right leg and sweeps Uke down with a sharp Osoto-gari.
Kodokan Key Point: Inside temple block & Osoto-gari — step inside the rotational arc of the strike to execute the reap cleanly.
TachiaiUnarmedTemple ChopOsoto-gari
15
見込 / 蹴上 Ke-age "Groin Kick (Standing)"

Uke advances and delivers a right front snap kick toward Tori's groin. Tori steps back-right, scoops Uke's kicking heel from underneath with the left hand, lifts the leg high, pushes Uke's shoulder, and throws Uke down.

1.Uke steps forward, launching a right front snap kick aimed directly at Tori's testicles.
2.Tori turns the body slightly sideways, steps back with the right foot, and scoops Uke's right heel from underneath with the left forearm.
3.Tori hoists the leg upward while driving the right palm into Uke's right chest/shoulder (Sukui-nage takedown).
Kodokan Key Point: Heel scooping — step off the centerline and scoop the heel upward before pushing the chest.
TachiaiUnarmedKick DefenseLeg Scoop Takedown
16
突込 Tsukikomi "Straight Dagger Thrust (Standing)"

Uke draws the dagger and lunges forward with a straight thrust to Tori's abdomen. Tori pivots off-line, parries Uke's right wrist, strikes Uke's eyes, applies an armlock over the shoulder, and disarms the blade.

1.Uke draws the dagger from the belt and lunges forward with a straight abdominal stab.
2.Tori pivots back-left (Tai-sabaki), parries Uke's right wrist with the left hand, and strikes Uke's eyes with the right open hand (Me-tsubushi).
3.Tori applies Waki-gatame across Uke's right elbow, brings Uke prone to the tatami, and strips the knife cleanly.
Kodokan Key Point: Eye strike (Me-tsubushi) — flick the fingertips toward Uke's eyes to disrupt concentration before locking the arm.

17
切込 Kiri-komi "Overhead Dagger Cut (Standing)"

Uke raises the dagger overhead with reverse grip and delivers a downward diagonal slash at Tori's neck. Tori steps inside, blocks Uke's wrist, delivers a palm strike to Uke's chin, and executes Kote-gaeshi to disarm.

1.Uke raises the dagger overhead in reverse grip and steps forward with a downward ice-pick slash at Tori's left neck.
2.Tori steps forward-left inside the blow, intercepts Uke's right wrist with the left hand, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho).
3.Tori twists Uke's wrist outward in Kote-gaeshi, throws Uke backward, and disarms the dagger.
Kodokan Key Point: Inside entry & Kote-gaeshi — enter deep inside the blade's downward trajectory to prevent full acceleration.
TachiaiDagger (Tantō)Overhead SlashKote-gaeshi Disarm
18
抜掛 Nuki-kake "Sword Draw Jam (Katana)"

Uke begins to draw the samurai sword (Katana) from the scabbard. Tori steps in preemptively before the sword is drawn, jams Uke's right hand on the hilt, strikes Uke's face, steps behind, and throws Uke backward.

1.Uke grasps the sword hilt (Tsuka) with the right hand and begins drawing the blade from the scabbard.
2.Before the blade clears the scabbard, Tori steps forward with the right foot, pins Uke's right hand/scabbard with the left hand, and strikes Uke's face with a right palm heel.
3.Tori wraps the right arm around Uke's neck/torso, steps deep behind Uke's right leg, throws Uke backward with Osoto-gari, and extracts the sword.
Kodokan Key Point: Preemptive draw jam (Sen-no-sen) — pin the sword hilt against the scabbard before the blade is drawn.
TachiaiSword (Katana)Preemptive JamOsoto-gari Disarm
19
切下 Kiri-oroshi "Overhead Sword Cut (Katana)"

Uke raises the drawn Katana overhead and executes a two-handed downward cut (Jodan-kiri). Tori steps forward-left off the cutting line, intercepts Uke's forearms, drives a palm strike to Uke's chin, and trips Uke with Osoto-gari to seize the sword.

1.Uke raises the drawn Katana high overhead with both hands and advances with a powerful downward cut.
2.Tori steps diagonally forward-left (Tai-sabaki) off the cutting line, blocks Uke's right forearm with the left hand, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel.
3.Tori steps behind Uke's right leg with Osoto-gari, throws Uke flat to the tatami, strips the Katana, and holds the blade over Uke.
Kodokan Key Point: Off-line evasion — step diagonally outside the descent line of the sword blade before striking the chin.

20
切込 Kiri-komi "Advancing Sword Slash (Katana)"

The 20th and final technique of Kime-no-Kata. Uke advances with a continuous diagonal sword slash. Tori ducks inside beneath the blade, wraps Uke's sword arm, drives a palm strike to the chin, executes a hip throw, and finishes with the sword.

1.Uke steps forward aggressively, delivering a diagonal sweeping slash with the Katana toward Tori's left temple/neck.
2.Tori steps deeply inside the arc of the sword with the right foot, checks Uke's forearms with the left hand, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel.
3.Tori wraps the right arm around Uke's waist, pivots, throws Uke with a hip throw (O-goshi / Uki-goshi), takes the Katana, and points the blade at Uke to conclude the kata.
Kodokan Key Point: Finale disarm & zanshin — throw Uke cleanly over the hips, disarm the Katana, and maintain focused martial awareness (Zanshin).

Principles & Etiquette

Kodokan Reihō & Weapon Etiquette

Kime-no-Kata preserves formal samurai etiquette, weapon maintenance, and decisive combative awareness.

Weapon Placement (Buki no Reihō)

Uke brings the Tantō (dagger) and Katana (sword) onto the tatami. The weapons are placed neatly to Uke's right side, cutting edges facing inward toward Uke. Uke retrieves the Tantō for Set 2 and Set 4, and the Katana for the final techniques.

Shinken Shobu (Real Combat Focus)

Every technique is executed with realistic combat intent (Shinken Shobu). Tori's counter-strikes (Atemi) to vital points (Kyūsho) must be crisp, decisive, and stopped cleanly 2–3 cm before anatomical injury.

Opening & Closing Protocol

Tori and Uke begin 5.5 meters apart. They perform standing bow (Ritsurei) to Joseki, face each other, assume Seiza, perform seated bow (Zarei), and begin Set 1 in seated Seiza posture.
