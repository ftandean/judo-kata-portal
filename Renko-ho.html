Introduction

What is Renko-hō?

Renko-hō (連行法) translates directly to "Escort Methods" or "Methods of Leading/Conducting Away." In Japanese martial arts—particularly in Taiho-jutsu (police arrest techniques), Judo Goshin-jutsu, and traditional Jujutsu—Renko-hō focuses on securing compliance through pain-compliance joint locks without inflicting permanent anatomical harm.

Unlike standard throwing or finishing techniques designed for immediate incapacitation, Renko-hō techniques are structured around three fundamental operational phases:
1. Capture & Neutralization (Kuzushi & Kansetsu): Securing an arm, wrist, or shoulder joint while destroying the subject's base.
2. Structural Locking (Katame): Locking the joint (elbow hyper-extension, shoulder hyper-rotation, or wrist flexion) so resistance causes self-inflicted discomfort.
3. Forward Escort Motion (Hoko-seigyo): Compelling the subject to walk forward while maintaining superior tactical posture and situational awareness.

Practitioners utilize body alignment (Tai-sabaki) and center-line dominance to lead aggressive or non-compliant individuals safely into custody.

Structure

Three Primary Vectors of Escort Control

Escort techniques are systematically grouped by the anatomical fulcrum leveraged to compel forward compliance.

VECTOR 01 — WRIST & HAND

Kote & Tekubi Renko

小手・手首連行 — Wrist Hyperflexion

Manipulations of the carpal joint (Kote-gaeshi, Kote-hineri, Kote-kudaki) forcing the suspect to stand on their toes and march forward.

  • 1. Kote-hineri Renko Wrist Twist Escort
  • 2. Kote-ori Renko Wrist Press Escort
VECTOR 02 — ELBOW & ARM

Hiji & Ude Renko

肘・腕連行 — Arm Extension & Entanglement

Hyperextending or pinning the elbow joint against the officer's chest, armpit, or forearm (Waki-gatame, Ude-gatame).

  • 3. Waki-gatame Renko Armpit Armlock Escort
  • 4. Ude-garami Renko Entangled Arm Escort
VECTOR 03 — REAR SHOULDER

Kata & Ushiro Renko

肩・後方連行 — Rear Shoulder Immobilization

Dominating from the blind spot by locking the subject's arm behind their back (Hammerlock / Kakae-kubi) for maximum retention.

  • 5. Ushiro-ude Renko Rear Chicken-Wing Escort
  • 6. Kubi-kakae Renko Head & Arm Escort Hold

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

Filter by control vector to examine the mechanical lever, escort grip, and compliance mechanics.

01
小手捻連行 Kote-hineri Renko "Inward Wrist-Twist Escort"

Tori applies an inward rotational twist on Uke's wrist (similar to Aikido Ikkyo / Yonkyo principles), anchoring the elbow against Tori's ribs and driving Uke forward under steady pain compliance.

1.Tori secures Uke's right wrist with both hands, twisting it inward while stepping to Uke's flank.
2.Tori pulls Uke's arm across the torso, locking Uke's elbow firmly against Tori's hip or ribcage.
3.Maintaining upward pressure on the wrist and downward pressure on the elbow, Tori steps forward to guide Uke.
Kodokan / Taiho Point: Keep Uke's wrist flexed toward their forearm; any slack allows Uke to counter-rotate or pull back.
Wrist VectorKote-hineriInward TorqueFlank Position
02
小手折連行 Kote-ori Renko "Wrist Hyperflexion (Goose Neck) Escort"

Tori forces Uke's hand into maximum palm-to-forearm flexion (Nikyo / goose-neck lock), lifting Uke onto the balls of the feet and compelling continuous forward locomotion.

1.From a frontal or side intercept, Tori folds Uke's wrist downwards into acute flexion.
2.Tori reinforces the lock with both hands, pinning Uke's elbow close to Uke's centerline to neutralize striking reach.
3.Tori applies upward-forward torque, compelling Uke to step forward to alleviate pressure on the carpal joint.
Kodokan / Taiho Point: Raising Uke's center of gravity makes sudden resistance or kicking impossible.
Wrist VectorHyperflexionNikyo LockPain Compliance
03
脇固連行 Waki-gatame Renko "Armpit Armlock Escort Hold"

Tori traps Uke's extended arm beneath the armpit, applying hyperextension across the elbow joint while holding Uke's wrist with both hands to march them forward.

1.Tori parries or secures Uke's straight arm, clamping Uke's wrist and clamping the elbow under Tori's armpit.
2.Tori lifts the wrist while bearing down with the chest/torso onto the triceps tendon.
3.Tori walks forward synchronously with Uke, ready to take Uke directly to the mat if resistance occurs.
Kodokan / Taiho Point: Hyperextension angle — maintain the thumb pointed upward to keep the elbow joint aligned for full leverage.
Elbow VectorWaki-gatameStraight ArmlockTakedown Transition
04
腕緘連行 Ude-garami Renko "Entangled Armlock (Keylock) Escort"

Tori bends Uke's arm at a 90-degree angle and threads a figure-four lock (Ude-garami / Kimura grip), dominating the shoulder rotation from the flank.

1.Tori captures Uke's wrist with the matching hand and threads the opposite forearm under Uke's triceps.
2.Tori grips their own wrist to form a secure figure-four lock while pressing Uke's elbow against the flank.
3.Elevating the elbow slightly forces Uke to lean forward, enabling secure escorting.
Kodokan / Taiho Point: Figure-four isolation — keep Uke's hand behind the plane of their back to prevent counter-spinning.
Elbow VectorFigure-FourUde-garamiShoulder Rotation
05
後腕連行 Ushiro-ude Renko "Rear Hammerlock Escort"

The standard police escort lock. Tori draws Uke's arm behind the back in a hammerlock, controlling the scapula and spine from Uke's tactical blind spot.

1.Tori spins or redirects Uke, bending Uke's arm upward across the middle of the back.
2.Tori grips Uke's wrist with one hand and places the opposite forearm or hand on Uke's neck or shoulder.
3.Tori drives forward while maintaining body alignment positioned slightly behind Uke's hip.
Kodokan / Taiho Point: Scapular elevation — lifting the hand toward the shoulder blades instantly removes all offensive leverage from Uke's other limbs.
Shoulder VectorHammerlockRear FlankArrest Standard
06
首抱連行 Kubi-kakae Renko "Head & Arm Control Escort"

Used for highly combative or volatile subjects. Tori secures an underhook and wraps the opposite arm around the cervical spine, completely controlling head orientation and locomotion.

1.Tori steps beside Uke, wrapping one arm around Uke's neck while controlling the near wrist/elbow.
2.Tori locks Uke's posture bent slightly forward, redirecting Uke's balance into Tori's forward stride.
3.Tori navigates Uke forward through crowded or restricted areas with full physical containment.
Kodokan / Taiho Point: Head follows the spine — controlling the tilt of the head prevents Uke from turning to strike or bite.

Principles & Tactics

Renko-hō Tactical & Safety Principles

Effective custodial escort demands maintaining dominant position, 360-degree awareness, and instant escalation/de-escalation control.

Tactical Blind Spot (Position 2.5)

The practitioner never walks directly beside or in front of the subject. Positioning slightly behind the subject's hip ensures dominant leverage while preventing headbutts or elbows.

Continuous Pain Compliance

Pressure is calibrated so forward movement relieves pain while stopping or struggling immediately intensifies it, turning the subject's own instincts toward cooperation.

Zanshin & Environment Awareness

During transport, the practitioner maintains relaxed vision (Enzan no Metsuke) to detect secondary threats, obstacles, doorways, or vehicle transition points.
