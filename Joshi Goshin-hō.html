Introduction

What is Joshi Goshin-hō?

Joshi Goshin-hō (女子護身法), meaning "Women's Self-Defense Forms," was established in 1943 by a specialized committee within the Kodokan Women's Section (including pioneer instructors such as Keiko Fukuda and Noritomi Masako) to address common physical assaults targeting women.

The kata is composed of 18 techniques systematically organized into three pedagogical sets of six techniques each:
1. Toshu no bu — Front Grabs & Seizures (1–6): Defenses against wrist grabs, arm holds, lapel seizures, and frontal bear hugs.
2. Ushiro no bu — Rear Attacks, Chokes & Hair Pulls (7–12): Defenses against rear collar grabs, strangulations, hair seizures from behind, and rear bear hugs.
3. Atemi & Buki no bu — Strikes, Kicks & Weapon Threats (13–18): Defenses against incoming punches, slanting temple strikes, front kicks, club swings, and knife threats.

Rather than relying on brute muscular strength, Joshi Goshin-hō utilizes anatomical weak points, sharp vital point strikes (Atemi-waza), joint rotational leverage (Kansetsu-waza), and body shifting (Tai-sabaki) to allow smaller defenders to neutralize larger aggressors.

01
両手取 Ryote-dori "Both Wrists Seized"

Uke grabs both of Tori's wrists from the front. Tori steps back, drops the center of mass, rotates both wrists outward against Uke's thumbs where the grip is weakest, delivers a front kick to the groin, and escapes.

1.Uke steps forward and grips both of Tori's wrists firmly from the front.
2.Tori drops the hips in a stable stance and circles both wrists outward against Uke's thumbs to break the lock.
3.Tori delivers a sharp right front snap kick (Mae-geri) to Uke's groin (Kinteki) and steps back to safety.
Kodokan Key Point: Weakest point of the grip — rotate outward against Uke's thumbs to pop the wrists free instantly before kicking.

02
片腕取 Kataude-dori "Single Arm Seized"

Uke seizes Tori's right wrist with the left hand. Tori raises the right arm, steps inside, delivers a palm-heel strike to Uke's chin, and applies an armlock to take Uke to the tatami.

1.Uke seizes Tori's right wrist with the left hand.
2.Tori raises the right arm, steps forward-left inside Uke's guard, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho).
3.Tori traps Uke's right arm under the armpit in Waki-gatame and presses Uke to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Stepping inside — do not pull backward; step inside the attacker's reach to deliver the palm strike.

03
交差手取 Katate-dori (Kosa-dori) "Cross Wrist Seized"

Uke grabs Tori's right wrist with the right hand (same-side or cross-wrist grab). Tori grasps her own right fist with the left hand, steps in, and wrenches the wrist downward to break the grip.

1.Uke grabs Tori's right wrist with the right hand in a cross-grab.
2.Tori clasps her own right fist with her left hand, reinforcing the pulling power.
3.Tori drops the bodyweight and wrenches the arms downward across Uke's thumb joint to strip the grip.
Kodokan Key Point: Two-handed reinforcement — reinforce the trapped wrist with both hands to overpower a single-hand grab.
Set 1Front GrabsCross GrabTwo-Handed Break
04
帯取 Obi-dori "Belt Seized from Front"

Uke grabs Tori's jacket or belt from the front to pull her close. Tori strikes Uke's eyes with open fingertips, steps back, and kicks Uke's groin.

1.Uke reaches forward and grabs Tori's belt/jacket from the front.
2.Tori thrusts open fingertips sharply into Uke's eyes (Me-tsubushi) to blind and disorient.
3.As Uke recoils, Tori delivers a front kick to the groin and steps away.
Kodokan Key Point: Eye strike distraction — use a sudden finger jab to the eyes to break Uke's focus and grip instantly.
Set 1Front GrabsEye JabGroin Counter
05
胸取 Mune-dori "Lapel Seized"

Uke seizes Tori's lapel with both hands. Tori covers Uke's hands, stamps heavily on Uke's instep, and executes an outward wrist lock (Kote-gaeshi) to take Uke down.

1.Uke seizes both of Tori's lapels with two hands.
2.Tori covers Uke's hands with her own and stomps the heel firmly onto Uke's instep (Kori).
3.Tori grips Uke's right wrist, wrenches it outward in Kote-gaeshi, and throws Uke to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Instep stamp distraction — stomp the foot to make Uke flinch downward, loosening the lapel grip for the wrist lock.
Set 1Front GrabsLapel GrabKote-gaeshi
06
抱取 Kakae-dori "Front Bear Hug (Arms Trapped)"

Uke hugs Tori from the front, trapping Tori's arms against her torso. Tori drops the center of gravity, delivers a sharp knee strike (Hiza-geri) to Uke's groin/abdomen, and pushes Uke away.

1.Uke wraps both arms around Tori's torso from the front, pinning Tori's arms.
2.Tori drops the hips in a wide base, leans the upper body back slightly to create clearance.
3.Tori drives a powerful right knee upward into Uke's groin or solar plexus (Hiza-geri), breaking the bear hug.
Kodokan Key Point: Knee strike (Hiza-geri) — use close-range knee strikes to the groin when arms are pinned and punches are impossible.
Set 1Front GrabsBear Hug EscapeKnee Strike
07
後襟取 Ushiro-eri-dori "Collar Seized from Behind"

Uke grabs Tori's collar from behind and pulls. Tori turns 180 degrees under the arm, delivers an elbow strike to Uke's ribs, and applies an armlock over the shoulder.

1.Uke grabs Tori's collar from behind and pulls backward.
2.Tori raises the arm, spins 180 degrees beneath Uke's arm, and drives a rear elbow strike (Ushiro-ate) into Uke's ribs.
3.Tori locks Uke's arm across the shoulder and forces Uke down to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Spinning under the collar grab — ducking cleanly beneath Uke's arm while countering with the elbow strike.
Set 2Rear AttacksCollar GrabElbow Counter
08
後絞 Ushiro-jime "Rear Choke / Strangle"

Uke applies a rear naked choke (Hadaka-jime). Tori pulls down on Uke's forearm to relieve airway pressure, steps back, delivers a rear foot stomp, and throws Uke over the shoulder.

1.Uke applies a rear choke around Tori's neck.
2.Tori immediately pulls down on Uke's forearm with both hands to breathe, stomps hard on Uke's foot, and bends forward.
3.Tori drops hips and throws Uke forward over the shoulder in a shoulder throw.
Kodokan Key Point: Immediate airway relief — pull down on the choking arm before attempting any foot stomp or throw.
Set 2Rear AttacksChoke DefenseShoulder Throw
09
髪取 Kami-tsukareta baai "Hair Pull from Behind"

Uke grabs Tori's hair from behind. Tori places both hands firmly over Uke's hand to lock the hair against her head (preventing scalp tearing), steps back, and executes a rear armlock or throw.

1.Uke seizes Tori's hair from behind, pulling upward.
2.Tori instantly places both hands over Uke's hand, pressing it firmly against her own head to lock the hair roots in place and prevent tearing.
3.Tori steps back, pivots under Uke's arm, and applies a wrist/arm lock to bring Uke down.
Kodokan Key Point: Locking the hair against the head — never pull away from a hair grab; press Uke's hand to your head first to protect the scalp.
Set 2Rear AttacksHair Pull DefenseScalp Protection
10
後抱取 Ushiro-kakae-dori "Rear Bear Hug (Arms Free)"

Uke hugs Tori from behind with arms free. Tori drops the center of mass heavily, strikes backward with the elbow to Uke's solar plexus, and throws Uke with a major outer reap (Osoto-gari).

1.Uke wraps arms around Tori's torso from behind (arms free).
2.Tori drops the hips heavily to anchor the base, and drives a sharp rear elbow strike (Ushiro-ate) into Uke's ribs.
3.Tori steps deep behind Uke's leg and executes Osoto-gari to throw Uke backward.
Kodokan Key Point: Dropping base & elbow strike — sink the hips instantly to prevent being lifted, and strike backward with the elbow.
Set 2Rear AttacksRear Bear HugOsoto-gari
11
後片腕取 Ushiro-kataude-dori "Single Arm Seized from Behind"

Uke grabs Tori's right wrist from behind. Tori pivots 180 degrees, delivers a palm-heel strike to Uke's chin, and applies an armlock over the shoulder.

1.Uke grabs Tori's right wrist from behind and pulls.
2.Tori pivots 180 degrees clockwise, closing distance, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel (Teisho).
3.Tori locks Uke's arm across the shoulder and forces Uke down to the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Inward pivot — pivot directly toward the trapped arm to neutralize the pull and strike the chin.
Set 2Rear AttacksWrist Grab BehindTeisho Counter
12
後押 Ushiro-oshi "Push from Behind"

Uke pushes Tori aggressively from behind. Tori steps back to match the push, pivots as Uke overextends, and applies a corner drop (Sumi-otoshi) or leg sweep to throw Uke forward.

1.Uke shoves Tori aggressively forward from behind.
2.Tori steps forward with the shove, pivots 180 degrees to face Uke, and catches Uke's advancing arms.
3.Tori redirects Uke's forward momentum into a corner drop (Sumi-otoshi), projecting Uke face-down onto the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Riding the push — step forward with the push to maintain balance, then pivot to redirect the forward momentum.
Set 2Rear AttacksPush DefenseSumi-otoshi
13
斜打 Naname-uchi "Slanting Temple Strike"

Uke delivers a diagonal downward hammer-fist strike to Tori's temple. Tori steps inside, blocks Uke's forearm, strikes Uke's chin with a palm heel, and throws Uke with Osoto-gari.

1.Uke steps forward delivering a diagonal hammer-fist strike toward Tori's left temple.
2.Tori steps forward-left inside the blow, blocks Uke's forearm with the left hand, and drives a right palm heel (Teisho) to Uke's chin.
3.Tori steps deep behind Uke's right leg and executes Osoto-gari to take Uke down.
Kodokan Key Point: Inside entry & Osoto-gari — step inside the striking arc and execute the reap immediately.
Set 3Strikes & WeaponsTemple StrikeOsoto-gari
14
顔面突 Gammen-tsuki "Face Punch Defense"

Uke throws a straight punch to Tori's face. Tori steps back-left (Tai-sabaki), parries the punch, delivers a right palm strike to Uke's chin, and applies Kote-gaeshi.

1.Uke advances with a straight right punch to Tori's face.
2.Tori steps back-left off the attack line, parries Uke's wrist, and strikes Uke's chin with a right palm heel.
3.Tori grips Uke's wrist, twists it outward in Kote-gaeshi, and brings Uke down to the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Off-line parry & Kote-gaeshi — step off the centerline and convert the parry into an outward wrist lock.

15
前蹴 Mae-geri "Front Kick Defense"

Uke delivers a front kick toward Tori's abdomen. Tori steps back, scoops Uke's kicking heel from underneath with the forearm, elevates the leg, and pushes Uke's shoulder to drop him.

1.Uke steps forward delivering a right front snap kick toward Tori's midsection.
2.Tori steps back with the right foot, turning slightly sideways, and scoops Uke's right heel upward with the left forearm.
3.Tori elevates the leg while pushing Uke's chest/shoulder with the right palm (Sukui-nage takedown).
Kodokan Key Point: Scooping the heel — catch the kicking leg underneath the heel to break Uke's standing balance.
Set 3Strikes & WeaponsFront Kick CatchLeg Scoop
16
棒振下 Bo-furioroshi "Club / Stick Overhead Swing Defense"

Uke raises a wooden club or stick overhead and swings it down at Tori's head. Tori steps diagonally forward-left off the path of the club, strikes Uke's chin, and executes Osoto-gari to disarm the club.

1.Uke raises a club high overhead and swings it downward toward Tori's head.
2.Tori steps diagonally forward-left (Tai-sabaki) outside the club's path, blocking Uke's right arm.
3.Tori strikes Uke's chin with a palm heel, steps behind Uke's leg, and executes Osoto-gari to throw Uke and disarm the club.
Kodokan Key Point: Stepping outside the swing — clear the head completely from the descending arc of the club before countering.
Set 3Strikes & WeaponsClub DefenseOsoto-gari Disarm
17
短刀突掛 Tanto-tsukkake "Knife Thrust Defense"

Uke thrusts a knife (Tantō) directly toward Tori's abdomen. Tori pivots off the line of the blade, parries Uke's wrist, locks the elbow into Waki-gatame, forces Uke down, and disarms the knife.

1.Uke draws a knife and thrusts straight toward Tori's abdomen.
2.Tori shifts the hips back-left off the blade's line, parries Uke's right wrist with the left hand, and strikes Uke's face with the right hand.
3.Tori applies Waki-gatame across Uke's right elbow, brings Uke prone to the tatami, and strips the knife.
Kodokan Key Point: Body off the blade path — turn the torso completely away from the knife's line before locking the arm.
Set 3Strikes & WeaponsKnife DefenseWaki-gatame Disarm
18
背面付 Haimen-zuke "Threat from Behind / Finale"

The 18th and concluding technique of Joshi Goshin-hō. Uke threatens Tori from behind. Tori raises hands, pivots 180 degrees swiftly, knocks the weapon/arm away, locks Uke's arm, and takes control to conclude the kata.

1.Uke threatens Tori from behind with a weapon or grab.
2.Tori raises both hands in surrender, then swiftly pivots 180 degrees clockwise on the left foot, deflecting Uke's arm outward with the right elbow.
3.Tori traps Uke's arm, applies an armlock, takes Uke to the mat, and maintains Zanshin control to conclude the kata.

Kodokan Key Point: Explosive 180-degree pivot — rotate the body smoothly on the ball of the foot to clear the threat trajectory instantly.

Principles & Leverage

Joshi Goshin-hō Leverage & Atemi Principles

Mastery of women's self-defense relies on biomechanical advantage rather than muscular confrontation.

Anatomical Weak Points

Joshi Goshin-hō targets vulnerable points (Kyusho) such as the eyes, chin, ribs, groin, and joints, allowing a smaller defender to generate immediate flinch reflexes in a larger aggressor.

Leverage Over Muscle

Techniques like Kote-gaeshi and Waki-gatame use two-handed rotational torque against single finger joints or elbows, maximizing mechanical advantage.

Opening & Closing Reihō

Partners stand 5.5m apart. They perform standing bow (Ritsurei) to Joseki, face each other, drop to Seiza for seated bow (Zarei), rise, and step forward with the left foot into Shizentai.
