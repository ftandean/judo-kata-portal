Introduction

What is Katame-waza Ura-no-Kata?

Katame-waza Ura-no-Kata (固技裏の形), often referred to simply as Katame-ura or Mifune's groundwork counter forms, is the "reverse" or counter-offensive companion to the official Kodokan Katame-no-Kata. Developed under the direction of 10th Dan master Kyuzo Mifune, it preserves high-level ne-waza escapes and instantaneous turnovers.

While the standard Katame-no-Kata shows Tori successfully controlling Uke across 15 techniques, the Ura-no-Kata flips the narrative: as Tori attempts to lock in each classic technique, Uke exploits minute structural lapses, posture angles, or timing gaps to turn the tables.

The kata is composed of 15 reversal techniques organized into three symmetrical sets of five:
1. Osaekomi-waza Counters (1–5): Escapes and sweeps against pins (Kesa, Kata, Kami-shiho, Yoko-shiho, Kuzure-kami-shiho).
2. Shime-waza Counters (6–10): Defensive counters, airway reliefs, and neck reversals against chokes (Kata-juji, Hadaka, Okuri-eri, Kata-ha, Gyaku-juji).
3. Kansetsu-waza Counters (11–15): Armlock roll-throughs, bridge escapes, and counter-locks (Ude-garami, Juji-gatame, Ude-gatame, Hiza-gatame, Ashi-garami).

01
袈裟固の裏 Kesa-gatame Ura "Counter to Scarf Hold"

As Tori secures Hon-kesa-gatame, Uke joins hands around Tori's torso, pulls Tori's center of gravity backward, bridges explosively over the left shoulder, and rolls Tori completely onto the back.

1.Tori attempts to settle into Hon-kesa-gatame around Uke's neck and right arm.
2.Uke immediately locks both hands behind Tori's waist and shifts hips beneath Tori's center of gravity.
3.Uke executes an explosive bridge (Bridge & Roll) over the shoulder, rolling Tori over to reverse the top pin.
Mifune Key Point: Get your hips directly under the opponent's center of gravity before bridging to ensure zero dead weight.

02
肩固の裏 Kata-gatame Ura "Counter to Shoulder Hold"

As Tori locks the head and arm in Kata-gatame, Uke joins hands, locks Tori's near leg with both feet, bridges toward Tori's head, and sweeps Tori over.

1.Tori traps Uke's right arm across the throat and steps out into Kata-gatame.
2.Uke clasps both hands together to create breathing space and hooks Tori's trailing leg with the feet.
3.Uke turns diagonally into Tori and bridges hard, upending Tori's base and rolling onto the top position.
Mifune Key Point: Trap the trailing leg to prevent Tori from widening the base to absorb the bridge.

03
上四方固の裏 Kami-shiho-gatame Ura "Counter to Upper Four-Corner Hold"

Tori pins Uke from the top in Kami-shiho-gatame. Uke secures Tori's belt on both sides, swings the legs to create momentum, bridges upward, and twists Tori over the shoulder.

1.Tori grips Uke's belt on both sides from above the head in Kami-shiho-gatame.
2.Uke grips Tori's belt firmly, swings both legs to one side, and builds rotational momentum.
3.Uke bridges high onto one shoulder while turning the hips, throwing Tori overhead onto the back.
Mifune Key Point: Use pendulum momentum of the legs in sync with a violent hip bridge.

04
横四方固の裏 Yoko-shiho-gatame Ura "Counter to Side Four-Corner Hold"

Tori attempts Yoko-shiho-gatame across Uke's chest. Uke inserts the near knee under Tori's abdomen, frames against the neck, shrimp-turns (Ebi), and recovers the closed guard or sweeps.

1.Tori moves in perpendicular across Uke's chest to set Yoko-shiho-gatame.
2.Before Tori locks down, Uke frames the forearm against Tori's jaw and shrimps the hips out.
3.Uke threads the bottom knee inside, kicks through to off-balance Tori, and rolls on top.
Mifune Key Point: Immediate hip shrimp (Ebi) before Tori secures chest-to-chest contact.

05
崩上四方固の裏 Kuzure-kami-shiho-gatame Ura "Counter to Modified Upper Four-Corner Hold"

Tori secures Kuzure-kami-shiho with one arm underhooked. Uke traps the underhooking arm, bridges diagonally toward the trapped side, and rolls Tori over.

1.Tori attacks with Kuzure-kami-shiho-gatame, underhooking Uke's shoulder.
2.Uke clamps down on Tori's underhooking arm, preventing Tori from posting.
3.Uke bridges violently toward that trapped side, flipping Tori over and establishing top control.
Mifune Key Point: Trap the posting limb tightly so the opponent has no support on that quadrant.

06
片十字絞の裏 Kata-juji-jime Ura "Counter to Half Cross Strangle"

Tori applies Kata-juji-jime from the mount. Uke pushes up on Tori's crossing elbow to relieve the choke, wedges the forearm inside, bridges, and rolls Tori into the guard.

1.Tori crosses the hands on Uke's lapels in Kata-juji-jime.
2.Uke places both hands on Tori's top elbow, pushing it upward to open the airway.
3.Uke traps Tori's leg on the same side, bridges diagonally, and sweeps Tori over.
Mifune Key Point: Relieve the trachea immediately by driving the top elbow across before initiating the sweep.

07
裸絞の裏 Hadaka-jime Ura "Counter to Naked Choke"

Tori applies Hadaka-jime from behind. Uke pulls down on Tori's forearm with two hands, drops the chin, twists into Tori, and executes Seoi-nage over the shoulder.

1.Tori locks the forearm across Uke's throat in Hadaka-jime.
2.Uke immediately grabs Tori's choking wrist with both hands, pulling down firmly and tucking the chin.
3.Uke steps hips deep beneath Tori and throws Tori forward over the shoulder.
Mifune Key Point: Two-hand downward pull to create an airway pocket, transitioning directly into the forward throw.

08
送襟絞の裏 Okuri-eri-jime Ura "Counter to Sliding Lapel Strangle"

Tori applies Okuri-eri-jime from the rear. Uke controls the choking hand, spins 180 degrees inward toward Tori's chest, breaks the choke angle, and pins Tori from the guard.

1.Tori feeds the lapel around Uke's neck in Okuri-eri-jime.
2.Uke grabs Tori's choking sleeve and spins 180 degrees inward, facing Tori directly.
3.By turning chest-to-chest, Uke neutralizes the leverage of the lapel and establishes top posture.
Mifune Key Point: Spin toward the choking hand to unravel the lapel leverage instantly.

09
片羽絞の裏 Kata-ha-jime Ura "Counter to Single-Wing Strangle"

Tori lifts Uke's arm in Kata-ha-jime. Uke reaches back to grab Tori's elevated arm, bridges backwards onto Tori's chest, and applies a counter-armlock or turnover.

1.Tori threads the arm behind Uke's neck to lock Kata-ha-jime.
2.Uke reaches over with the opposite hand, trapping Tori's threading wrist.
3.Uke steps back and turns sharply, peeling Tori's arm into an armlock reversal.
Mifune Key Point: Trap the wing arm before Tori locks it behind the neck.

10
逆十字絞の裏 Gyaku-juji-jime Ura "Counter to Reverse Cross Strangle"

Tori attacks with palms-up Gyaku-juji-jime. Uke reaches inside, peels Tori's wrists apart against the thumbs, bridges, and executes a classic reversal into top mount.

1.Tori sets four fingers inside both lapels in Gyaku-juji-jime.
2.Uke places both hands on Tori's inner wrists and wedges outward to strip the grip.
3.Uke drives hips upward, bridging Tori off-balance, and claims top mount.
Mifune Key Point: Strip the crossed wrists outward against the thumbs before the arms can flare.

11
腕緘の裏 Ude-garami Ura "Counter to Entangled Armlock"

Tori attacks with Ude-garami (Keylock / Kimura). Uke straightens the attacked arm immediately into a hitchhiker roll, spins 360 degrees, and escapes to top position.

1.Tori bends Uke's arm at 90 degrees and weaves the figure-four grip.
2.Before Tori elevates the shoulder, Uke straightens the arm and points the thumb outward.
3.Uke spins across Tori's body in a hitchhiker roll, clearing the shoulder joint and landing on top.
Mifune Key Point: Straighten the arm immediately — Ude-garami only functions when the elbow remains bent at 90 degrees.

12
腕挫十字固の裏 Ude-hishigi-juji-gatame Ura "Counter to Cross Armlock"

Tori sits back for Juji-gatame. Uke locks hands (Gable grip), stacks Tori heavily on the shoulders, pivots the hips, and pulls the elbow free to establish side control.

1.Tori steps over Uke's head and sits back for Juji-gatame.
2.Uke clasps hands tightly and immediately drives forward, stacking Tori's weight onto the upper spine.
3.Uke circles the head around Tori's leg, slips the elbow to the mat, and takes side control.
Mifune Key Point: Heavy stacking — never stay flat on your back; stack all your weight onto Tori's neck to kill hip extension.

13
腕挫腕固の裏 Ude-hishigi-ude-gatame Ura "Counter to Arm Armlock"

Tori clamps both hands onto Uke's straight arm in Ude-gatame. Uke rotates the arm so the elbow bends safely, underhooks Tori's leg, and rolls Tori into a sweep.

1.Tori traps Uke's extended arm against the neck/shoulder in Ude-gatame.
2.Uke rotates the forearm inward, turning the fulcrum of the elbow away from Tori's chest pressure.
3.Uke uses the free hand to underhook Tori's leg and bridges, sweeping Tori onto the back.
Mifune Key Point: Rotate the radial bone — changing the angle of the elbow joint neutralizes straight armbar pressure.

14
腕挫膝固の裏 Ude-hishigi-hiza-gatame Ura "Counter to Knee Armlock"

Tori applies Hiza-gatame using the knee as a fulcrum. Uke pushes Tori's knee downward with the free hand, steps the hips over the leg, and rolls Tori over.

1.Tori places the knee across Uke's elbow joint in Hiza-gatame.
2.Uke uses the free hand to push Tori's fulcrum knee off the elbow and across the centerline.
3.Uke steps around the displaced leg and drives forward, pinning Tori in Kuzure-kesa-gatame.
Mifune Key Point: Push the knee fulcrum off line before Tori can hyperextend the joint.

15
足緘の裏 Ashi-garami Ura "Counter to Entangled Leglock"

Tori attacks with the ancient Ashi-garami leg entanglement. Uke unwinds the controlling leg with both hands, turns the heel away from the lock, and applies a counter leg lock or back take.

1.Tori wraps the legs around Uke's knee and thigh in Ashi-garami.
2.Uke peels Tori's top hooking foot off the hip and clears the knee line.
3.Uke rotates through the entanglement, steps over Tori's hips, and locks the counter control.
Mifune Key Point: Clear the knee line — once the knee escapes the entanglement, all locking leverage collapses.

Principles & Reversals

Mifune's Ne-waza Counter Principles

Mastery of groundwork reversals depends on feeling the opponent's center of gravity and reacting without hesitation.

Kuzushi from Below

The defender under a pin must create unbalancing (Kuzushi) using hip movement (Ebi) and bridging (Bridging & Rolling) to make the top player light and unstable.

Preemptive Joint Alignment

Joint lock counters rely on rotating the limb (thumb up, radial rotation) before the hyperextension lock is fully engaged, neutralizing the locking fulcrum.

Omote to Ura Harmony

The kata demonstrates that every offensive groundwork technique has a natural structural counter, embodying Kano's principle of Ju-yoku-go-o-seisu (gentleness overcomes hardness).
