Introduction

What is Nanatsu-no-Kata?

Nanatsu-no-Kata (七つの形), meaning "The Seven Forms," represents one of the higher-level conceptual kata in traditional Judo and classical budo. Sharing profound philosophical kinship with the Itsutsu-no-Kata (Forms of Five) and Tenri/Kosen historical training modules, it explores the interaction between opposing energies, gravity, centrifugal acceleration, and effortless yield.

Rather than serving as a catalogue of self-defense tricks, the kata is a kinetic study of nature's forces across seven distinct movements categorized into three overarching energetic phases:
1. Linear & Inertial Momentum (Forms 1–2): Yielding to continuous direct drive and converting linear push into circular vacuum.
2. Centrifugal & Rotational Vectors (Forms 3–5): Channeling spiral energy, tidal ebb-and-flow, and centrifugal projection.
3. Equilibrium & Dissipation (Forms 6–7): Absolute harmonization, sphere of influence control, and void dissipation.

Through smooth, unresisting execution, Tori demonstrates Seiryoku Zen'yo (Maximum Efficiency with Minimum Effort) by moving in complete synchrony with Uke's momentum.

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

Explore the biomechanical laws, force vectors, and execution timing for each of the seven forms.

01
第一本：直進と順応 Dai-ippon (Chokushin to Jun'ō) "Direct Drive & Yielding Compliance"

Uke initiates a direct, powerful forward advance pushing into Tori's chest. Instead of bracing against the force, Tori yields completely backward step-by-step, matching Uke's velocity until Uke overextends, leading to an effortless float and takedown.

1.Uke steps forward purposefully with both hands gripping Tori, applying constant linear forward force.
2.Tori maintains upright posture and retreats with matching cadence, offering zero resistance to the push.
3.At maximum forward extension, Tori drops hips slightly and redirects the momentum downward, causing Uke to pitch forward onto the tatami.
Core Principle: Ju-yoku-go-o-seisu — when pushed, give way immediately. Never oppose direct momentum; accelerate it to exhaustion.
Phase 1Linear ForceYieldingInertia
02
第二本：吸引と反転 Dai-nihon (Kyūin to Hanten) "Vacuum Draw & Dynamic Reversal"

Uke attempts to pull Tori sharply into their space. Tori glides forward into the vacuum created by Uke's pull, instantly overtaking Uke's center of gravity and projecting Uke backward as their balance collapses.

1.Uke grips Tori's lapels and steps backward vigorously to drag Tori off-balance.
2.Tori does not pull back; rather, Tori steps forward faster than Uke anticipates, entering the vacuum.
3.Tori expands the chest and drives through Uke's centerline, flattening Uke backward onto the mat.
Core Principle: When pulled, step forward and fill the space. Use the attacker's own vacuum to overthrow their base.
Phase 1Vacuum EntryCenterline DominanceReversal
03
第三本：遠心渦流 Dai-sanbon (Enshin Karyū) "Centrifugal Vortex Projection"

Tori initiates a circular rotation around a stationary central axis. Uke is drawn into the perimeter of the orbit where centrifugal acceleration multiplies, resulting in a soaring rotational projection.

1.Engaging grips, Tori pivots smoothly on the ball of the lead foot, establishing an unshakeable vertical axis.
2.Uke is drawn into circular motion around Tori, unable to regain linear footing as the rotational radius narrows.
3.Tori extends the arms along the tangent line of the circle, unleashing centrifugal force to throw Uke smoothly across the tatami.
Core Principle: The eye of the hurricane — remain perfectly still and balanced at the center while projecting the opponent along the swirling perimeter.
Phase 2Centrifugal ForceVortex OrbitRotational Dynamics
04
第四本：潮汐の波乱 Dai-yonhon (Chōseki no Haran) "Tidal Surge & Ebb (The Wave)"

Expressing the unstoppable rhythm of ocean waves. Tori absorbs Uke's incoming momentum like water receding from the shoreline, then returns instantly as a rising tidal swell to unbalance and cast Uke away.

1.Uke surges forward with heavy surging momentum.
2.Tori sinks and retreats like an ebbing tide, absorbing all kinetic impact into the hips and legs.
3.Without a pause, Tori surges upward and forward like a breaking wave, lofting Uke cleanly onto the back.
Core Principle: Fluid recoil — like a wave crashing against cliffs, water absorbs impact and rebounds with equal and opposite force.
Phase 2Wave RhythmTidal SurgeElastic Recoil
05
第五本：螺旋展開 Dai-gohon (Rasen Tenkai) "Diagonal Spiral Projection"

Combining vertical lift with horizontal twisting to generate a corkscrew vector. Tori blends with Uke's advance, twisting upward along a 3D helix path that completely neutralizes Uke's ability to base out.

1.Uke approaches diagonally with forceful intent.
2.Tori steps along the diagonal angle (Tai-sabaki), intertwining arms and corkscrewing the body upward.
3.The upward-spiral energy lifts Uke's heels off the ground before projecting them along the spiral descent.
Core Principle: Three-dimensional helical projection — spiral mechanics eliminate all resistance planes by attacking vertical and horizontal balances simultaneously.
Phase 2Spiral VectorHelix Lift3D Tai-sabaki
06
第六本：球体調和 Dai-roppon (Kyūtai Chōwa) "Sphere of Equilibrium & Balance"

Tori maintains an invisible spherical field of balance. Regardless of the angle, speed, or direction Uke attacks from, Tori rotates the sphere seamlessly, remaining completely upright while Uke rolls off the surface.

1.Uke tests Tori from various angles with fluctuating pushes, pulls, and grips.
2.Tori stays relaxed in Shizentai, rolling the shoulders and hips like a ball bearing to deflect every vector.
3.Uke slips off Tori's curvature, overbalancing under their own weight and tumbling to the ground.
Core Principle: Spherical non-resistance — a sphere has no corners to catch or push against; it rolls effortlessly with incoming pressure.
Phase 3Spherical BodyZero FrictionEffortless Yield
07
第七本：虚空消散 Dai-nanahon (Kokū Shōsan) "Void Dissipation & Supreme Stillness"

The pinnacle of Nanatsu-no-Kata. When Uke strikes or rushes forward with maximum aggression, Tori completely dissolves all physical presence and resistance, causing Uke's energy to dissipate harmlessly into the void (Kū).

1.Uke unleashes a full-commitment charge or strike toward Tori's center.
2.Tori shifts with absolute economy of motion, leaving an empty space where target used to be.
3.Uke falls into the boundless emptiness, crashing forward under unspent momentum while Tori concludes in calm Zanshin.
Core Principle: Mu (Void) — when the defender offers no substance or opposition, the attacker's own violent force becomes their sole undoing.
Phase 3The Void (Kū)Supreme ZanshinKata Finale

Philosophy & Kinetics

Philosophical & Kinetic Principles

Nanatsu-no-Kata translates physical combat into a demonstration of universal kinetic laws and Zen philosophy.

Seiryoku Zen'yo (Maximum Efficiency)

The forms prove that physical strength is unnecessary when aligning perfectly with universal laws of inertia, centrifugal torque, and gravity.

Ju-no-Ri (Principle of Softness)

Yielding is not passive submission; it is the active mastery of vector alignment where soft, adaptable water erodes hard, brittle rock.

Zanshin & Dynamic Stillness

The kata starts in quiet stillness, builds through elemental vortexes and tidal surges, and returns to absolute mental and physical calm.
