Introduction

What is Kaeshi-no-Kata?

Kaeshi-no-Kata (返しの形 / 講道館返し技の形), meaning "Forms of Counter Techniques," is the master pedagogical study of counter-throwing (Kaeshi-waza / Gaeshi-waza) in Kodokan Judo. It provides the structured bridge between fundamental static throws and dynamic free sparring (Randori).

The kata is organized into 15 representative counter-throws divided into three sets of five:
1. Ashi-waza no Kaeshi (足技の返し): Counters against foot sweeps and reaps (Deashi-harai, Osoto-gari, Ouchi-gari, Kouchi-gari, and Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi).
2. Koshi-waza no Kaeshi (腰技の返し): Counters against forward hip entries and wheeling turns (O-goshi, Harai-goshi, Hane-goshi, Tsurikomi-goshi, and Koshi-guruma).
3. Te-waza & Sutemi-waza no Kaeshi (手技・捨身技の返し): Counters against shoulder throws, body drops, and sacrifice entries (Seoi-nage, Tai-otoshi, Kata-guruma, Tomoe-nage, and Ura-nage).

The overarching timing principle governing Kaeshi-no-Kata is Go-no-sen (後の先): taking the late initiative. Tori allows Uke to initiate an attack, senses the balance commitment, executes flawless body management (Tai-sabaki) or an immovable pelvic block, and reverses the momentum before Uke can recover.

Structure

The Three Counter Sets

Techniques progress from foot sweep counters and hip reversals into advanced sacrifice counter-projections.

SET 01 — 5 TECHNIQUES

Ashi-waza no Kaeshi

足技の返し — Foot Technique Counters

Counters against foot sweeps and leg reaps using timing, foot lifts, re-reaping, and hip shifts.

  • 1. Deashi-harai → Tsubame-gaeshi Swallow Flight Counter
  • 2. Osoto-gari → Osoto-gaeshi Major Outer Reversal
  • 3. Ouchi-gari → Ouchi-gaeshi Major Inner Reversal
  • 4. Kouchi-gari → Kouchi-gaeshi Minor Inner Reversal
  • 5. Sasae → Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi Counter Propping Ankle
SET 02 — 5 TECHNIQUES

Koshi-waza no Kaeshi

腰技の返し — Hip Technique Counters

Counters against hip entries using pelvic blocks, counter-hoisting, and outer wraparounds.

  • 6. O-goshi → Utsuri-goshi Shifting Hip Counter
  • 7. Harai-goshi → Harai-gaeshi Sweeping Hip Reversal
  • 8. Hane-goshi → Hane-goshi-gaeshi Spring Hip Reversal
  • 9. Tsurikomi-goshi → Ushiro-goshi Rear Hip Counter
  • 10. Koshi-guruma → Ura-nage Rear Suplex Counter
SET 03 — 5 TECHNIQUES

Te & Sutemi no Kaeshi

手技・捨身技の返し — Hand & Sacrifice Counters

Counters against shoulder throws, body drops, and sacrifice entries using hopping evasion and drops.

  • 11. Seoi-nage → Yoko-guruma Side Wheel Counter
  • 12. Tai-otoshi → Kouchi-gari Inner Leg Trip Counter
  • 13. Kata-guruma → Sumi-gaeshi Corner Reversal Counter
  • 14. Tomoe-nage → Osaekomi-waza Pass into Scarf Hold
  • 15. Ura-nage → Sukui-nage Scooping Drop Counter

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

Each of the 15 counter techniques detailed with Uke's initial attack vector and Tori's decisive counter execution.

01
燕返 Deashi-harai → Tsubame-gaeshi "Swallow Flight Counter against Foot Sweep"

Uke steps forward to sweep Tori's advancing right foot with Deashi-harai. Tori senses the sweep, floats the right foot over Uke's sweeping leg like a swallow in flight, and instantly sweeps Uke's supporting left ankle to project Uke.

1.Uke retreats and attempts Deashi-harai, sweeping toward Tori's advancing right ankle.
2.Tori lifts the right foot smoothly just over Uke's sweeping foot, causing Uke's sweep to hit empty air.
3.Before Uke can recover balance, Tori sweeps Uke's standing left leg with the right foot while pulling the sleeve and lapel to throw Uke flat to the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Floating clearance — step over Uke's sweeping foot cleanly without stiffening, using the downward momentum to counter-sweep immediately.

02
大外返 Osoto-gari → Osoto-gaeshi "Major Outer Reversal against Outer Reap"

Uke steps deep to attack Tori with Osoto-gari. Tori braces the left support leg, pulls Uke's lapel and sleeve downward to absorb the impact, and reaps Uke's supporting left leg with the right leg to throw Uke backward.

1.Uke steps deep alongside Tori's right foot, swinging the right leg to execute Osoto-gari.
2.Tori lowers the center of gravity, pulls down on Uke's collar, and shifts bodyweight onto the left foot to prevent being driven back.
3.As Uke commits forward momentum, Tori drives forward and reaps Uke's supporting left leg with the right leg to execute Osoto-gaeshi.
Kodokan Key Point: Stance anchorage — anchor the lead foot and use Uke's forward chest commitment to drive through Uke's support base.
Set 1Ashi-wazaOsoto-gaeshiOuter Reap Counter
03
大内返 Ouchi-gari → Ouchi-gaeshi "Major Inner Reversal against Inner Reap"

Uke steps inside and hooks the right leg deeply to reap Tori's left inner leg with Ouchi-gari. Tori shifts weight off the targeted leg, drives the upper body into Uke's chest, and sweeps Uke's supporting leg backward.

1.Uke drives forward, stepping between Tori's legs to reap Tori's left leg with Ouchi-gari.
2.Tori lifts the left heel slightly and pulls Uke's upper torso tightly to the chest, neutralizing Uke's reaping leverage.
3.Tori drives forward with both hands and sweeps Uke's supporting left leg backward (or executes Sukui-nage / Deashi-harai counter) to topple Uke.
Kodokan Key Point: Unweighting the reaped leg — shift weight to the free leg and counter with direct forward chest drive.
Set 1Ashi-wazaOuchi-gaeshiInner Reap Counter
04
小内返 Kouchi-gari → Kouchi-gaeshi "Minor Inner Reversal against Inner Reap"

Uke enters with a sliding foot to reap Tori's right inner heel with Kouchi-gari. Tori retracts the right heel, steps over Uke's sweeping leg, and sweeps Uke's supporting leg with Deashi-harai / Sukui-nage.

1.Uke slides the right foot along the mat to reap the inside of Tori's right heel with Kouchi-gari.
2.Tori lifts the right foot over Uke's sweeping leg, stepping it down firmly behind Uke's center.
3.Tori drives both hands forward-downward and sweeps Uke's supporting leg to throw Uke to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Heel retraction — lift the targeted foot just above the mat before Uke connects, stepping in to counter immediately.
Set 1Ashi-wazaKouchi-gaeshiRetraction Counter
05
支釣込足返 Sasae → Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi "Counter Propping Ankle against Propping Ankle"

Uke attempts Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi, blocking Tori's right ankle with the left foot. Tori steps over Uke's propped foot, pivots to the flank, and props Uke's remaining support foot to wheel Uke over.

1.Uke retreats and props the left foot against Tori's advancing right ankle.
2.Tori bends the right knee, steps over Uke's propping foot, and pivots the hips to the left.
3.Tori lifts high with the right lapel, pulls the sleeve, and props the right foot against Uke's right ankle to execute Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi.
Kodokan Key Point: Stepping over the prop — evade the propped ankle, re-anchor the lead foot, and apply the counter-prop with high collar lift.
Set 1Ashi-wazaMutual SasaeAnkle Prop Reversal
06
移腰 O-goshi → Utsuri-goshi "Shifting Hip Counter against Major Hip Throw"

Uke turns in deeply to attack with O-goshi. Tori drops into a deep stance, blocks Uke's hip entry with the pelvis, hoists Uke straight up with both arms around the waist, shifts Uke onto the hip, and throws Uke over.

1.Uke pivots 180 degrees and wraps Tori's waist to execute O-goshi.
2.Tori bends both knees, drives the pelvis forward into Uke's lower back, and wraps both arms tightly around Uke's waist.
3.Tori extends the legs to hoist Uke into the air, shifts Uke onto Tori's right hip, and rolls Uke forward to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Pelvic check & shift — block Uke's hips before the lift begins, hoist Uke off the tatami, and transfer Uke onto the counter-hip.

07
払腰返 Harai-goshi → Harai-gaeshi "Sweeping Hip Reversal against Sweeping Hip Throw"

Uke inserts the hip and extends the right leg to sweep Tori with Harai-goshi. Tori leans into Uke's back, braces the support leg, hooks the right leg behind Uke's sweeping leg, and re-sweeps Uke over.

1.Uke enters for Harai-goshi, sweeping the right leg against Tori's right outer thigh.
2.Tori shifts weight forward against Uke's back, preventing the forward projection.
3.Tori hooks Uke's sweeping leg from behind with the right leg (or executes Ushiro-goshi / Ushiro-guruma) to throw Uke backward.
Kodokan Key Point: Counter-sweep leverage — press tightly against Uke's back to kill Uke's rotation, then sweep Uke's standing support leg.
Set 2Koshi-wazaHarai-gaeshiCounter Sweep
08
跳腰返 Hane-goshi → Hane-goshi-gaeshi "Spring Hip Reversal against Spring Hip Throw"

Uke enters for Hane-goshi, springing the bent leg against Tori's inner thigh. Tori sinks the hips, lifts the targeted leg over Uke's springing knee, and sweeps Uke's supporting leg to throw Uke down.

1.Uke enters for Hane-goshi, bending the right leg to spring against Tori's inner thigh.
2.Tori lowers the center of gravity, steps the targeted leg around Uke's springing knee, and presses the chest into Uke's back.
3.Tori sweeps Uke's supporting left leg with the right foot to execute Hane-goshi-gaeshi (or Osoto-gari).
Kodokan Key Point: Evading the spring — avoid Uke's springing leg by stepping around it and immediately attacking the single standing leg.
Set 2Koshi-wazaHane-goshi-gaeshiSupport Leg Sweep
09
後腰 Tsurikomi-goshi → Ushiro-goshi "Rear Hip Counter against Lifting Hip Throw"

Uke drops deep to lift Tori with Tsurikomi-goshi. Tori drops hips even lower, wraps both arms around Uke's waist from behind, arches the spine, and hoists Uke straight backward to the mat.

1.Uke drops the hips deeply between Tori's legs to execute Tsurikomi-goshi.
2.Tori sinks the hips lower than Uke's belt line and clamps Uke's waist from behind with both hands.
3.Tori drives the hips upward, lifting Uke straight into the air, and drops backward to slam Uke onto the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Lower hip level — Tori must get lower than Uke's lowest drop to generate direct vertical hoisting power.
Set 2Koshi-wazaUshiro-goshiRear Hoist Counter
10
裏投 Koshi-guruma → Ura-nage "Rear Suplex Counter against Hip Wheel"

Uke wraps the right arm around Tori's neck to execute Koshi-guruma. Tori steps deep behind Uke's flank, wraps both arms around Uke's torso, arches backward into a high bridge, and throws Uke with Ura-nage.

1.Uke wraps the arm around Tori's neck and pivots to execute Koshi-guruma.
2.Tori steps the left foot deep behind Uke's left heel, wrapping both arms around Uke's waist and chest.
3.Tori drops weight backward, thrusts the hips upward into a full bridge, and launches Uke backward over the left shoulder.
Kodokan Key Point: Stepping behind the neck wrap — as Uke reaches around the neck, step deep behind the flank to secure the rear suplex leverage.

11
横車 Seoi-nage → Yoko-guruma "Side Wheel Counter against Shoulder Throw"

Uke turns in for Seoi-nage. Tori steps over Uke's shoulder, wraps both arms around Uke's waist, slides the right leg deep between Uke's legs, falls onto the side, and wheels Uke over in a sacrifice throw.

1.Uke pivots 180 degrees and wedges the elbow into Tori's armpit to execute Seoi-nage.
2.Tori leans over Uke's back, wrapping both arms tightly around Uke's chest/waist.
3.Tori slides the right leg between Uke's thighs, drops onto the left hip, and wheels Uke in a large diagonal circle (Yoko-guruma).
Kodokan Key Point: Sliding leg insertion — insert the leg deep between Uke's thighs as you fall sideways to create the wheeling axis.
Set 3Yoko-sutemiYoko-gurumaShoulder Throw Counter
12
小内刈 Tai-otoshi → Kouchi-gari "Minor Inner Reap Counter against Body Drop"

Uke extends the right leg across to execute Tai-otoshi. Tori steps the left foot over Uke's extended leg, slides the right foot inside, and reaps Uke's supporting left heel with Kouchi-gari.

1.Uke pulls and extends the right leg wide across Tori's shins for Tai-otoshi.
2.Tori steps the right foot lightly over Uke's extended leg, neutralizing the hand pull.
3.Tori drives forward with both hands, sliding the right foot to reap Uke's supporting left heel from the inside with Kouchi-gari.
Kodokan Key Point: Stepping over the extended leg — hop or step over Uke's blocking leg and immediately reap the single loaded support leg.
Set 3Ashi-wazaKouchi-gariTai-otoshi Counter
13
隅返 Kata-guruma → Sumi-gaeshi "Corner Reversal against Shoulder Wheel"

Uke ducks low between Tori's legs to hoist Kata-guruma. Tori grips Uke's rear belt over the back, drops straight down, hooks the instep between Uke's legs, and flips Uke diagonally over.

1.Uke bends knees deeply and slides the arm between Tori's thighs to lift Kata-guruma.
2.Before Uke can stand, Tori reaches deep over Uke's back to grip the belt.
3.Tori sits straight down, slides the right instep under Uke's thigh, and rolls backward to flip Uke in Sumi-gaeshi.
Kodokan Key Point: Deep belt grip & drop — capture the belt before Uke straightens the legs, using Uke's low posture to flip Uke overhead.
Set 3Ma-sutemiSumi-gaeshiKata-guruma Counter
14
袈裟固 Tomoe-nage → Osaekomi-waza "Guard Pass into Scarf Hold against Stomach Throw"

Uke drops underneath to execute Tomoe-nage. Tori pushes Uke's placing foot off the abdomen, sidesteps Uke's roll, and drops directly into Kesa-gatame / Yoko-shiho-gatame on the mat.

1.Uke slides the foot to Tori's abdomen and drops backward to execute Tomoe-nage.
2.Tori brushes Uke's foot off the stomach with the hand, stepping the right foot wide to the side.
3.As Uke lands flat on the back, Tori drops chest-to-chest, wraps Uke's neck, and locks Uke in Kesa-gatame.
Kodokan Key Point: Foot brush & sidestep — clear the foot from the abdomen before Uke can extend the leg, moving straight into ground control.

15
掬投 Ura-nage → Sukui-nage "Scooping Drop Counter against Rear Suplex"

The 15th and concluding technique. Uke attempts Ura-nage from behind Tori's flank. Tori sinks the hips, turns into Uke, scoops Uke's thighs from behind, and dumps Uke to the mat.

1.Uke steps behind Tori's flank and wraps the waist to hoist Ura-nage.
2.Tori drops the hips low in Jigotai, turns slightly inward to face Uke, and reaches both hands behind Uke's knees/thighs.
3.Tori scoops Uke's legs upward while driving the body forward, dumping Uke backward onto the mat to conclude the kata.
Kodokan Key Point: Low hip anchor & leg scoop — drop below Uke's arch and scoop both thighs to reverse the suplex throw.

Principles & Mechanics

Kodokan Counter-Throwing Theory

Mastery of counter techniques requires understanding timing, body evasion, and balance commitment.

Go-no-Sen (後の先) Timing

Go-no-sen is the art of late initiative: allowing the opponent to commit fully to their attack vector, neutralizing their force at the point of no return, and countering with decisive speed.

Tai-sabaki & Pelvic Blocking

Effective counters never rely on backing away. Tori uses circular footwork (Tai-sabaki) to step around sweeps or drives the pelvis into Uke's hips to kill rotational momentum.

Opening & Closing Reihō

Partners stand 5.5m apart. They perform standing bow (Ritsurei) to Joseki, face each other, perform seated bow (Zarei), rise, and advance with natural stepping (Ayumi-ashi) into Migi-shizentai.
