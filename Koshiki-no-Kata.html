Introduction

What is Koshiki-no-Kata?

Koshiki-no-Kata (古式の形), literally "Forms of Antiquity," was originally the secret core kata of Kito-ryu Jujutsu (known as Kito-ryu no Kata). Jigoro Kano preserved this kata intact within Kodokan Judo because it embodies the ultimate expression of Ju (yielding), balance, and unbalancing (Kuzushi).

The kata consists of 21 techniques arranged into two contrasting sets: Omote (表 — Front Set, 14 techniques) and Ura (裏 — Back / Rear Set, 7 techniques).

Omote is performed assuming both warriors are clad in heavy traditional samurai armor (Katchu / Yoroi). The footwork is measured, stable, and deliberate (Suri-ashi with low center of mass), emphasizing balance shifts and projecting the armored adversary over fulcrums.

Ura represents unarmored, swift hand-to-hand engagements where the movements become continuous, rapid, dynamic, and explosive.

Structure

The Two Sets (Omote & Ura)

The kata transitions from heavy, deliberate armored grappling mechanics into fast, fluid unarmored combat.

SET 01 — 14 TECHNIQUES

Omote (Front Set)

表の形 — Armored Combat (Slow & Heavy)

Executed assuming full samurai battlefield armor. Movements are slow, stately, deliberate, and maintain centered posture without leaning or folding at the waist.

  • 1. Tai Ready Stance / Posture
  • 2. Yume-no-uchi Within a Dream
  • 3. Ryoku-hi Avoiding Force
  • 4. Mizu-guruma Water Wheel
  • 5. Mizu-nagare Flowing Water
  • 6. Hiki-otoshi Draw Down
  • 7. Ko-daore Log Falling
  • 8. Uchi-kudaki Smashing Down
  • 9. Tani-otoshi Valley Drop
  • 10. Kuruma-daoshi Wheel Throw
  • 11. Shikoro-dori Helmet Flange Grip
  • 12. Shikoro-gaeshi Helmet Flange Twist
  • 13. Yudachi Summer Rainshower
  • 14. Taki-otoshi Waterfall Drop
SET 02 — 7 TECHNIQUES

Ura (Rear Set)

裏の形 — Unarmored Combat (Fast & Fluid)

Executed with speed, agility, and continuous motion, representing light, unarmored combat where momentum is turned instantaneously against the attacker.

  • 15. Mi-kudaki Body Smash

  • 18. Ryu-setsu Snow on Willow

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

All 21 techniques detailed strictly according to the official Kodokan Judo textbook standard.

01
体 Tai "Ready Posture / Body Stance"

The opening ceremonial engagement. Tori and Uke advance in heavy armored posture (Katchu-no-kamae), engage right lapel and sleeve grips, step three paces, and return to balanced posture.

1.Both assume upright natural posture, stepping forward with stately, deliberate footwork (Ayumi-ashi).
2.They take right grips with arms held rounded (as if wearing bulky shoulder armor — Sode).
3.They walk three paces forward, halt, test each other's balance and centered tension, and smoothly disengage.
Kodokan Key Point: Armored posture — rounded arms and centered weight without collapsing the frame under armor.
OmoteArmored PostureStance Calibration
02
夢の内 Yume-no-uchi "Within a Dream"

Walking forward together, Uke suddenly grasps Tori's left collar to strike. Tori steps back, yields to the push, pulls Uke forward onto the toes, and projects Uke forward to the tatami.

1.Both advance together in right grip. Uke attempts to initiate an attack by pushing forward.
2.Tori retreats three paces in Tsugi-ashi, drawing Uke into overextension.
3.Tori drops the left knee/hip, pulling both arms downward in a circular trajectory to throw Uke forward (Uki-otoshi variation).
Kodokan Key Point: Effortless yield — Uke is cast down without realizing how balance was lost, as if in a dream.
OmoteLinear YieldForward Projection
03
力避 Ryoku-hi "Avoiding Force"

Uke pushes heavily with full armored weight. Tori steps back diagonally, absorbs the momentum, slips behind Uke, and sweeps Uke down backward.

1.Uke drives forward with heavy physical pushing force through both arms.
2.Tori steps diagonally back-right with the right foot (Tai-sabaki), letting Uke's linear drive plunge into empty space.
3.Tori places the left palm behind Uke's left shoulder, sweeps the foot, and throws Uke backward onto the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Evading direct force — redirecting the armored adversary's linear momentum off-line.
OmoteDiagonal StepRear Takedown
04
水車 Mizu-guruma "Water Wheel"

Tori pulls Uke in a circular path, ducks deep beneath Uke's center of mass, hoists Uke across the shoulders, and rotates Uke onto the mat like a revolving water wheel.

1.Tori leads Uke in a wide circular walking turn, drawing Uke's weight forward onto the right toes.
2.Tori sinks hips deeply beneath Uke's armored belt line, inserting the right shoulder under Uke's chest.
3.Tori lifts Uke over the back and wheels Uke smoothly to the tatami (Kata-guruma variation).
Kodokan Key Point: Wheel elevation — lifting the armored opponent using leg power rather than back strain.
OmoteShoulder WheelCircular Hoist
05
水流 Mizu-nagare "Flowing Water"

Uke steps forward to deliver an overhead blow. Tori yields fluidly, ducks underneath the strike like flowing water around a rock, and throws Uke over the back.

1.Uke raises the right fist/sword hand to strike down at Tori's head.
2.Tori sinks the body, steps forward-left beneath the blow, and clamps Uke's right arm.
3.Tori extends the hips and projects Uke forward over the shoulder (Seoi-nage / flowing drop).
Kodokan Key Point: Fluid ducking — flowing under the attack without breaking the vertical spine alignment.
OmoteFlowing YieldShoulder Projection
06
曳落 Hiki-otoshi "Draw Down / Pulling Drop"

Both move backward and forward. Tori suddenly steps back, grips Uke's lapels, drops the left knee to the mat, and pulls Uke straight down in a steep diagonal arc.

1.Tori and Uke engage in right grip, taking three coordinated advancing steps.
2.Tori breaks Uke's forward balance, dropping the left knee straight to the tatami.
3.Tori pulls both arms downward and backward, casting Uke onto the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Downward drop — timing the knee drop to coincide with Uke's forward stepping foot.
OmoteKnee DropStraight Pull
07
虚倒 Ko-daore "Log Falling / Collapse"

Uke pushes with rigid arms. Tori steps back, drops underneath, scoops Uke's legs, and topples Uke backward like a felled log.

1.Uke advances with rigid, stiff arms pushing against Tori's chest.
2.Tori yields on the third step, ducks down, and grabs behind both of Uke's knees (Morote-gari / leg scoop).
3.Tori drives the shoulder into Uke's abdomen while lifting the legs, toppling Uke flat on the back.
Kodokan Key Point: Rigid collapse — capitalizing on Uke's stiff, unbending armored posture to fell the legs.
OmoteLeg TackleRigid Topple
08
打砕 Uchi-kudaki "Smashing Down"

Uke steps in to seize Tori's throat. Tori intercepts Uke's arms, lifts Uke by the waist/belt, and slams Uke downward onto the tatami.

1.Uke steps forward aggressively reaching for Tori's throat/collar.
2.Tori steps inside, wraps both arms around Uke's waist beneath the armor plates, and hoists Uke off the ground.
3.Tori drives Uke straight downward onto the mat with decisive bodyweight impact.
Kodokan Key Point: Waist lift & smash — lifting Uke completely off the ground before driving downward.
OmoteWaist LiftDownward Slam
09
谷落 Tani-otoshi "Valley Drop"

Tori draws Uke forward, steps deep behind both of Uke's heels, drops straight down to the side, and drops Uke backward as if over a precipice.

1.Tori leads Uke in right grip, pulling Uke forward onto the heels.
2.Tori steps the left foot deep behind Uke's heels, sliding the right leg across Uke's calves.
3.Tori drops weight directly to the mat, pulling Uke backward down into the void.
Kodokan Key Point: Rear sacrifice — sliding the leg behind both heels to create an unavoidable backward drop.
OmoteValley DropRear Leg Block
10
車倒 Kuruma-daoshi "Wheel Throw / Overturning Wheel"

Uke pushes in deep defensive posture. Tori steps deep between Uke's feet, drops flat onto the back, places the foot on Uke's lower abdomen, and flips Uke overhead (classical Tomoe-nage).

1.Uke pushes forward in a heavy defensive armored stance.
2.Tori slides the left foot deep between Uke's feet, sitting close to Uke's heels.
3.Tori places the right foot on Uke's abdomen, falls backward, and flips Uke overhead in a circular wheel.
Kodokan Key Point: Overhead stomach throw — dropping directly beneath Uke's center of gravity to flip the armored weight.
OmoteStomach ThrowOverhead Flip
11
錣取 Shikoro-dori "Helmet Flange Grip"

Tori reaches around Uke's head, grips the neck armor / helmet flange (Shikoro) with both hands, twists the head, and takes Uke down backward.

1.Uke steps in close to grapple; Tori slips behind Uke's left side.
2.Tori grasps both sides of Uke's helmet neck-guard (Shikoro) or shoulders with both hands.
3.Tori twists Uke's neck/helmet upward and backward, forcing Uke to collapse onto the back.
Kodokan Key Point: Helmet neck-guard leverage — twisting the helmet's neck-guard to control the cervical spine.
OmoteHelmet ControlNeck Twist
12
錣返 Shikoro-gaeshi "Helmet Flange Reversal"

Uke attempts to grab Tori's helmet neck-guard. Tori catches Uke's hands, rotates the wrists against the joints, and throws Uke backward.

1.Uke reaches with both hands to grab Tori's helmet flange.
2.Tori intercepts Uke's wrists from underneath, twisting them outward.
3.Tori steps back, applying double wrist torque (Kote-gaeshi variation) to throw Uke to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Reversing the helmet grab — intercepting and countering Uke's reaching hands before they lock onto the helmet.
OmoteWrist CounterReversal
13
夕立 Yudachi "Summer Rainshower"

Uke steps forward to strike. Tori steps in swiftly, lifts Uke by the jacket/chest, and brings Uke crashing down like a sudden sudden summer rainstorm.

1.Uke advances with pushing pressure; Tori steps in with right foot.
2.Tori lifts Uke's torso with both hands, breaking Uke's base completely.
3.Tori sweeps the supporting foot and slams Uke down decisively.
Kodokan Key Point: Sudden downpour — the lift and downward throw occur in one rapid, continuous burst of momentum.
OmoteSudden LiftRapid Slam
14
瀧落 Taki-otoshi "Waterfall Drop"

The 14th and concluding technique of the Omote set. Tori pulls Uke into deep forward unbalance, turns, hoists Uke high over the back, and plunges Uke downward like a cascading waterfall.

1.Tori leads Uke into deep forward unbalance in Tsugi-ashi.
2.Tori steps the right foot across, pivots 180 degrees, and lifts Uke across the back.
3.Tori drops forward, driving Uke straight down to the mat to conclude the armored set.
Kodokan Key Point: High waterfall projection — lifting Uke to maximum elevation before the vertical descent.
OmoteHigh LiftOmote Finale
15
身砕 Mi-kudaki "Body Smash"

Opening the Ura set with unarmored speed. Uke rushes in; Tori instantly pivots, traps Uke's incoming arm, and smashes Uke to the tatami in a rapid continuous transition.

1.Uke rushes forward at high speed without armor.
2.Tori pivots swiftly on the left foot, intercepting Uke's right arm.
3.Tori applies immediate downward rotational body torque, smashing Uke flat to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Instantaneous speed — transition from Omote's deliberate pace to Ura's lightning-fast unarmored timing.
UraUnarmored SpeedBody Smash
16
車返 Kuruma-gaeshi "Wheel Reversal"

As Uke pushes vigorously, Tori drops instantly to the mat, hooks the foot to Uke's stomach, and flips Uke overhead with lightning speed.

1.Uke attacks with rapid forward momentum.
2.Tori drops straight down without hesitation, planting the right foot on Uke's abdomen.
3.Tori launches Uke in a fast, seamless overhead arc (rapid Tomoe-nage).
Kodokan Key Point: Rapid sacrifice — dropping instantly under Uke's running attack without slowing down.
UraFast WheelOverhead Flip
17
水入 Mizu-iri "Entering the Water / Plunging"

Tori draws Uke into rapid forward steps, ducks low beneath Uke's center like diving into water, and launches Uke forward.

1.Tori leads Uke in fast, accelerating forward steps.
2.Tori ducks low between Uke's legs, lifting Uke's thighs onto the shoulders.
3.Tori stands and projects Uke forward into a rolling fall.
Kodokan Key Point: Plunging entry — diving beneath the opponent's momentum like plunging into deep water.
UraLow DiveFast Shoulder Hoist
18
柳雪 Ryu-setsu "Snow on the Willow"

Embodying the core proverb of Judo (yielding like a willow branch under heavy snow). Tori bends and yields to Uke's push, allowing the force to slide off, and drops Uke to the mat.

1.Uke charges forward with heavy pushing momentum.
2.Tori bends and yields completely to the force, stepping aside like a snow-laden willow branch releasing snow.
3.Uke's momentum carries him forward into complete balance loss, crashing to the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Pure yielding (Ju) — the philosophical heart of Judo; bending under weight until the attacker's own force causes their collapse.
UraPhilosophical CoreWillow Yield
19
坂落 Saka-otoshi "Headlong Fall / Slope Drop"

Tori maneuvers Uke into a rapid diagonal circle, places the hip across Uke's thighs, and drops Uke down a steep imaginary incline.

1.Tori spins Uke into a rapid circular turn.
2.Tori inserts the hip across Uke's lead leg while pulling the sleeve across.
3.Tori whips Uke over the hip in a steep, accelerating descent.
Kodokan Key Point: Accelerating slope — whipping the opponent over the hip like rolling down a steep hill.
UraHip WhipSteep Drop
20
雪折 Yuki-ore "Snow-Broken Branch"

Uke applies heavy downward pressing force. Tori absorbs the pressure, yields until maximum load, and suddenly releases the tension, causing Uke to break posture and plunge downward.

1.Uke presses downward heavily against Tori's shoulders/collar.
2.Tori sinks the center of gravity, yielding under the downward pressure.
3.Tori suddenly sidesteps and drops weight, pulling Uke down to the mat as if a branch snapped under snow.
Kodokan Key Point: Elastic snap — absorbing downward pressure until suddenly dropping the base to break Uke's equilibrium.
UraElastic YieldDownward Snap
21
岩波 Iwa-nami "Wave on the Rocks"

The 21st and final concluding technique of Koshiki-no-Kata. Uke surges forward like a tidal wave. Tori stands firm like an unyielding ocean rock, redirects the wave of energy around the body, and casts Uke away to conclude the kata.

1.Uke surges forward with a final, powerful full-body charge.
2.Tori stands rooted in centered posture, receiving the impact like a solid rock in the surf.
3.Tori turns the hips, channeling Uke's surging momentum past the flank and casting Uke onto the tatami with complete martial composure (Zanshin).
Kodokan Key Point: Kata finale & Zanshin — standing immovable like a bedrock while directing the surging wave of energy around you.
UraTidal RedirectionKata Finale

Lineage & Philosophy

Kito-ryu Heritage & Armored Principles

Koshiki-no-Kata preserves the pure mechanical origins of Kodokan Judo's yielding philosophy.

Kito-ryu Jujutsu Lineage

Koshiki-no-Kata is the direct preservation of Kito-ryu no Kata. Jigoro Kano mastered this system under masters Hachinosuke Fukuda and Tsunetoshi Iikubo, adopting its profound concepts of rising and falling (Ki-To).

Armored Kinematics (Katchu)

Omote requires maintaining a rounded, centered posture (Katchu-no-kamae). One does not bend at the waist or overextend the arms, as bulky helmet and shoulder plates would compromise balance.

Reihō & Protocol

Tori and Uke begin 5.5 meters apart. They perform Ritsurei toward Joseki, turn to face each other, perform Ritsurei, and advance with stately walking steps (Ayumi-ashi) into Tai (Posture 1).
