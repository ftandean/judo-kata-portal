Introduction

What is Go-no-Sen-no-Kata?

Go-no-Sen-no-Kata (後の先の形) is one of the most practical and combatively celebrated counter-throwing kata in Judo history. While rooted in early Kodokan technical research, it was refined and standardized through major pre-war high school and university programs (notably Waseda University and the Kosen Judo community under masters like Mikinosuke Kawaishi and Sumiyuki Kotani).

The kata is organized into 12 canonical counter-techniques divided into two sets of six:
1. Dai-ikkyo (第一教 — 6 Techniques): Counters against major outer and inner foot reaps, foot sweeps, and hip throws (Osoto-gari, Hiza-guruma, Ouchi-gari, Deashi-harai, Kosoto-gake, and Kouchi-gari).
2. Dai-nikyo (第二教 — 6 Techniques): Counters against forward hip entries, shoulder turns, and body drop mechanics (Kubi-nage/Koshi-guruma, Kosoto-gari, Hane-goshi, Harai-goshi, Uchi-mata, and Seoi-nage).

The philosophical and tactical core of the kata is Go-no-Sen (後の先): taking the late initiative. Tori does not preempt or stop the fight prematurely. Instead, Tori allows Uke to initiate a real attack, yields just enough to draw Uke past the point of balance recovery, and strikes back with an explosive counter-projection.

Structure

The Two Sets (12 Counter Throws)

The 12 canonical counters are divided into two equal groups of six techniques progressing from foot sweeps into major hip entries.

SET 01 — 6 COUNTERS

Dai-ikkyo (First Group)

第一教 — Foot Sweep & Inner Reap Reversals

Direct counters against linear foot sweeps, knee wheels, major and minor inner reaps, and outer hooks.

  • 1. Osoto-gari → Osoto-gaeshi Outer Reap Reversal
  • 2. Hiza-guruma → Hiza-guruma Counter Knee Wheel
  • 3. Ouchi-gari → Ouchi-gaeshi Inner Reap Reversal
  • 4. Deashi-harai → Tsubame-gaeshi Swallow Flight Counter
  • 5. Kosoto-gake → Tai-otoshi Outer Hook to Body Drop
  • 6. Kouchi-gari → Sasae-tsurikomi Inner Reap to Ankle Prop
SET 02 — 6 COUNTERS

Dai-nikyo (Second Group)

第二教 — Hip, Thigh & Shoulder Reversals

Dynamic counters against neck throws, outer reaps, spring hips, sweeping hips, inner thighs, and shoulder throws.

  • 7. Kubi-nage → Ushiro-goshi Neck Throw to Rear Hip Counter
  • 8. Kosoto-gari → Tai-otoshi Minor Outer Reap to Body Drop
  • 9. Hane-goshi → Sasae-tsurikomi Spring Hip to Ankle Prop
  • 10. Harai-goshi → Utsuri-goshi Sweeping Hip to Shifting Hip
  • 11. Uchi-mata → Sukui-nage Inner Thigh to Scooping Drop
  • 12. Seoi-nage → Sumi-gaeshi Shoulder Throw to Corner Reversal

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

Filter by group to explore the attack vector, unbalancing response, and counter execution for all 12 techniques.

01
大外返 Osoto-gari → Osoto-gaeshi "Counter Outer Reap against Major Outer Reap"

Uke steps in deeply to execute Osoto-gari. Tori lowers the center of gravity, firmly braces the left support foot, pulls down on Uke's collar and sleeve to neutralize the forward drive, and reaps Uke's supporting left leg with the right leg.

1.Uke steps deep alongside Tori's right foot, swinging the right leg to reap Tori with Osoto-gari.
2.Tori anchors the left foot firmly into the tatami, lowers the hips, and pulls Uke's upper body tightly inward to absorb the impact.
3.As Uke's forward drive stalls, Tori drives forward and reaps Uke's supporting left leg from behind, projecting Uke flat on the back.
Kodokan Key Point: Core anchorage — anchor the supporting base leg and use Uke's forward commitment to drive through Uke's lone standing leg.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaOsoto-gaeshiReap Counter
02
膝車返 Hiza-guruma → Hiza-guruma "Counter Knee Wheel against Knee Wheel"

Uke attempts Hiza-guruma, propping the left foot against Tori's right knee. Tori lifts the right leg over Uke's propped foot, steps down firmly, and immediately props the left foot against Uke's right knee to wheel Uke over.

1.Uke pulls in a circular arc and places the left foot against Tori's right knee to execute Hiza-guruma.
2.Tori bends the knee, steps the right foot lightly over Uke's blocking foot, and plants it outside Uke's base.
3.Tori rotates the steering wheel grip to the left, placing the left foot against Uke's right kneecap to complete the counter Hiza-guruma.
Kodokan Key Point: Stepping over the block — lift the knee over Uke's barrier without losing rotational momentum, reversing the steering wheel grip.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaHiza-gurumaStep-Over Counter
03
大内返 Ouchi-gari → Ouchi-gaeshi "Counter Inner Reap against Major Inner Reap"

Uke steps between Tori's legs to reap Tori's left inner leg with Ouchi-gari. Tori unweights the attacked leg, lifts the heel, drives the chest forward into Uke, and sweeps Uke's supporting leg backward.

1.Uke steps deeply inside and hooks the right leg to reap Tori's left inner thigh with Ouchi-gari.
2.Tori yields the left leg slightly, lifting the heel to avoid being pinned, and clamps Uke's upper body close.
3.Tori drives forward through Uke's center, sweeping Uke's supporting left leg with the left foot / hand drive to topple Uke backward.
Kodokan Key Point: Neutralizing the hook — lift the targeted heel to deny Uke friction on the mat, then drive forward with upper torso pressure.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaOuchi-gaeshiInner Reap Counter
04
燕返 Deashi-harai → Tsubame-gaeshi "Swallow Flight Counter against Foot Sweep"

Uke advances to sweep Tori's right advancing foot with Deashi-harai. Tori floats the right foot lightly over Uke's sweeping leg like a swallow skimming over water, and instantly sweeps Uke's standing left ankle.

1.Uke steps to time Deashi-harai against Tori's advancing right foot.
2.Tori senses the sweep, floats the right foot just above Uke's sole, allowing Uke to sweep empty air.
3.As Uke overextends on one leg, Tori applies the sole of the right foot to Uke's standing left ankle, sweeping Uke cleanly to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Weightless clearance — step over the sweep with zero tension, using the downward step to accelerate the counter-sweep.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaTsubame-gaeshiFoot Sweep Timing
05
体落 Kosoto-gake → Tai-otoshi "Body Drop Counter against Minor Outer Hook"

Uke steps in to hook Tori's right leg with Kosoto-gake. Tori unweights the hooked foot, pivots the hips 180 degrees, extends the right leg across Uke's shins, and throws Uke with a sharp Tai-otoshi.

1.Uke steps forward-right and hooks the right leg behind Tori's right ankle/calf in Kosoto-gake.
2.Tori pulls the foot free from the hook, steps the left foot back-left, and pivots the torso.
3.Tori extends the right leg wide across Uke's shins and drives with both hands to throw Uke in Tai-otoshi.
Kodokan Key Point: Hook disengagement & pivot — slip the foot out of the hook before weight settles, immediately rotating into the hand throw barrier.
Dai-ikkyoTe-wazaTai-otoshiHook Disengagement
06
支釣込足 Kouchi-gari → Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi "Propping Ankle Counter against Minor Inner Reap"

Uke slides the right foot along the tatami to reap Tori's right inner heel with Kouchi-gari. Tori retracts the heel over Uke's foot, steps down, lifts high with the lapel, and props Uke's advancing ankle with Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi.

1.Uke enters low, sliding the right foot to reap the inside of Tori's right heel with Kouchi-gari.
2.Tori lifts the right foot over Uke's sliding sweep, planting it firmly outside Uke's right foot.
3.Tori lifts upward with the right lapel (Tsurite), pulls the sleeve, and props the left foot against Uke's right ankle to wheel Uke forward.
Kodokan Key Point: Retract & prop — hop over the inner reap and convert Uke's forward momentum directly into an ankle wheel throw.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaSasae-tsurikomiInner Reap Counter
07
後腰 Kubi-nage → Ushiro-goshi "Rear Hip Counter against Neck Throw"

Uke wraps the right arm around Tori's neck to execute a headlock hip throw (Kubi-nage / Koshi-guruma). Tori lowers the center of gravity, clamps Uke's waist from behind, hoists Uke vertically into the air, and drops backward to slam Uke down.

1.Uke reaches around Tori's neck and turns the hips in to execute Kubi-nage.
2.Tori drops the hips lower than Uke's belt, braces the pelvis against Uke's lower back, and wraps both arms around Uke's waist.
3.Tori straightens the legs with explosive hip drive, lifting Uke straight off the mat, and drops backward to execute Ushiro-goshi.
Kodokan Key Point: Hip level leverage — sink lower than Uke's hip entry to generate straight vertical hoisting power before Uke's throw can rotate.
Dai-nikyoKoshi-wazaUshiro-goshiHeadlock Counter
08
体落 Kosoto-gari → Tai-otoshi "Body Drop Counter against Minor Outer Reap"

Uke attacks Tori's right heel with Kosoto-gari. Tori withdraws the right foot backward, pivots the body 180 degrees, extends the right leg wide across Uke's shins, and throws Uke forward with Tai-otoshi.

1.Uke steps outside to clip Tori's right heel with Kosoto-gari.
2.Tori pulls the right foot back, evading the reap, and steps the left foot back-left into a pivot.
3.Tori places the right leg horizontally across Uke's shins and drives with both hands to execute Tai-otoshi.
Kodokan Key Point: Heel evasion & hand drive — pull the targeted heel back cleanly and capitalize on Uke's forward momentum with Tai-otoshi.
Dai-nikyoTe-wazaTai-otoshiReap Evasion
09
支釣込足 Hane-goshi → Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi "Propping Ankle Counter against Spring Hip Throw"

Uke turns in to spring Tori with Hane-goshi. Tori drops the center of gravity, steps the targeted leg around Uke's springing knee, lifts high with the lapel, and props Uke's lone supporting ankle with Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi.

1.Uke turns in, bending the right leg to spring against Tori's inner thigh with Hane-goshi.
2.Tori sinks the hips, steps the right leg around Uke's bent knee, and pulls Uke's torso forward.
3.Tori lifts with the right collar, pulls the sleeve, and props the right foot against Uke's standing left ankle to wheel Uke over.
Kodokan Key Point: Circumventing the spring — step around the springing knee and attack the single loaded support leg while Uke is balancing on one foot.
Dai-nikyoAshi-wazaSasae-tsurikomiSpring Hip Counter
10
移腰 Harai-goshi → Utsuri-goshi "Shifting Hip Counter against Sweeping Hip Throw"

Uke turns in and extends the right leg to sweep Tori with Harai-goshi. Tori blocks Uke's hip rotation with an immovable pelvic check, wraps both arms around Uke's waist, hoists Uke into the air, shifts Uke onto the hip, and throws Uke.

1.Uke enters for Harai-goshi, sweeping the right leg against Tori's outer thigh.
2.Tori bends the knees, drives the pelvis into Uke's lower back to stop the sweep, and wraps the waist.
3.Tori straightens the legs to hoist Uke, shifts Uke onto Tori's forward right hip, and rolls Uke forward to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Pelvic stop & transfer — stop the sweeping leg with pelvic contact, elevate Uke off the floor, and transfer Uke onto the counter-hip.
Dai-nikyoKoshi-wazaUtsuri-goshiSweeping Hip Counter
11
掬投 Uchi-mata → Sukui-nage "Scooping Drop Counter against Inner Thigh Throw"

Uke enters for Uchi-mata, kicking the right leg high between Tori's thighs. Tori sidesteps the reap, reaches the right hand behind Uke's standing left thigh, scoops the leg upward, and drives Uke backward to the tatami.

1.Uke pivots 180 degrees and kicks the right leg high to reap Tori's inner thigh with Uchi-mata.
2.Tori shifts the hips back-left to slip the inner thigh away from Uke's kick.
3.Tori reaches the right arm behind Uke's standing left thigh, scoops the leg upward, and pushes Uke's upper torso backward to execute Sukui-nage.
Kodokan Key Point: Slipping the inner reap — shift the pelvis back to let Uke's kick pass through empty space, then scoop the standing support leg.
Dai-nikyoTe-wazaSukui-nageInner Thigh Counter
12
隅返 Seoi-nage → Sumi-gaeshi "Corner Reversal against Shoulder Throw"

The 12th and concluding counter of Go-no-Sen-no-Kata. Uke turns in low for Seoi-nage. Tori leans over Uke's back, wraps the belt over the shoulder, drops backward, hooks the instep between Uke's thighs, and flips Uke over in Sumi-gaeshi.

1.Uke pivots 180 degrees, dropping low to hoist Tori with Seoi-nage.
2.Tori leans forward over Uke's back, reaches deep over Uke's shoulder to grip the back belt, and neutralizes the throw.
3.Tori drops weight straight down, slides the right instep under Uke's inner thigh, and rolls onto the back to flip Uke diagonally in Sumi-gaeshi.
Kodokan Key Point: Deep belt grip & corner flip — capture the belt before Uke extends the legs, using Uke's low forward posture to flip Uke overhead.

Tactical Theory & Lineage

Go-no-Sen Tactical Timing & Heritage

Mastery of Go-no-Sen-no-Kata requires understanding the three initiatives of Japanese martial arts.

The Three Initiatives (Mitsu no Sen)

Sen-sen-no-sen: Preempting the attack before it begins. Sen-no-sen: Striking simultaneously at the moment of attack inception. Go-no-sen: Allowing the attack to launch, neutralizing it at the point of commitment, and countering.

Classical Lineage

Practiced widely across pre-war Kodokan branches, the Butokukai, and elite university dojos (Waseda, Kyoto Imperial University, and Tokyo Higher Normal School) to bridge the gap between static kata and Randori.

Opening & Closing Reihō

Partners stand 5.5m apart. They perform standing bow (Ritsurei) to Joseki, face each other, perform seated bow (Zarei), rise, step forward with the left foot into Migi-shizentai, and engage in continuous counter flow.
