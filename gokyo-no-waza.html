Introduction

What is the Gokyo-no-Waza?

Go-Kyo-no-Waza (五教の技) is the core throwing curriculum of Kodokan Judo. First organized in 1895 (42 techniques) and systematically refined in 1920 into its current standard of 40 techniques arranged into 5 groups (Kyo) of 8 throws each, it represents the primary pedagogical progression for learning standing judo (Nage-waza).

When performed in formal demonstration order as a structured sequence with standard opening and closing etiquette, it is practiced as Go-Kyo-no-Kata, testing a judoka's complete grasp of throwing mechanics, footwork (Tsugi-ashi and Ayumi-ashi), off-balancing vectors (Happō-no-kuzushi), and safe impact absorption (Ukemi).

The syllabus progresses logically from foundational upright sweeps and hip turns (Dai-ikkyo) through dynamic reaping and rotational throws (Dai-nikyo & Dai-sankyo) to powerful wheeling hip throws (Dai-yonkyo) and deep sacrifice projections (Dai-gokyo).

Structure

The Five Teachings (40 Throws)

The Gokyo is systematically organized into five distinct instructional groups (Dai-ikkyo to Dai-gokyo).

GROUP 01 — 8 THROWS

Dai-ikkyo

第一教 — The First Group (Foundations)

Fundamental upright throws teaching basic foot sweeps, hip turns, shoulder pivots, and outer reaps.

  • 1. Deashi-harai Advanced Foot Sweep
  • 2. Hiza-guruma Knee Wheel
  • 3. Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi Propping Ankle
  • 4. Uki-goshi Floating Hip
  • 5. Osoto-gari Major Outer Reap
  • 6. O-goshi Major Hip Throw
  • 7. Ouchi-gari Major Inner Reap
  • 8. Seoi-nage Shoulder Throw
GROUP 02 — 8 THROWS

Dai-nikyo

第二教 — The Second Group

Intermediate throws developing body rotation, circular motion, lateral foot sweeps, and inner sweeps.

  • 9. Kosoto-gari Minor Outer Reap
  • 10. Kouchi-gari Minor Inner Reap
  • 11. Koshi-guruma Hip Wheel
  • 12. Tsurikomi-goshi Lifting Hip Throw
  • 13. Okuri-ashi-harai Sliding Foot Sweep
  • 14. Tai-otoshi Body Drop
  • 15. Harai-goshi Sweeping Hip Throw
  • 16. Uchi-mata Inner Thigh Throw
GROUP 03 — 8 THROWS

Dai-sankyo

第三教 — The Third Group

Dynamic combination throws, hip sweeps, rear pickups, and foundational overhead sacrifice throws.

Step-by-Step

Technique Breakdown

Filter by group to explore the official Kodokan throwing mechanics, classifications, and execution keys for all 40 techniques.

01
出足払 Deashi-harai "Advanced Foot Sweep"

Tori draws Uke forward in Tsugi-ashi. As Uke advances the right foot before full weight is planted, Tori sweeps the right outer ankle across with the curved sole of the left foot.

1.Tori steps backward in Ayumi-ashi/Tsugi-ashi, drawing Uke to step forward with the right foot.
2.Tori pulls the left sleeve upward and lifts the right lapel, floating Uke's balance onto the toes.
3.Tori applies the sole of the left foot to Uke's right outer ankle, sweeping it cleanly across the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Sweep timing — sweep the foot at the exact microsecond before Uke's weight settles onto the heel.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaFoot Sweep
02
膝車 Hiza-guruma "Knee Wheel"

Tori draws Uke into a circular forward step, props the sole of the left foot against the outside of Uke's right kneecap, and wheels Uke forward in a large steering arc.

1.Tori steps back-left diagonally, pulling Uke forward-right in a circular trajectory.
2.Tori places the sole of the left foot lightly against the outer side of Uke's right knee (acting as a fulcrum, not kicking).
3.Tori rotates both hands like turning a large steering wheel, projecting Uke over the blocked knee.
Kodokan Key Point: Knee barrier — the foot serves as a stationary revolving pivot; upper body rotation drives the throw.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaKnee Wheel
03
支釣込足 Sasae-tsurikomi-ashi "Propping and Drawing Ankle Throw"

Tori retreats, draws Uke forward, steps to the side, blocks Uke's advancing ankle with the sole of the foot, and lifts/pulls with the hands to rotate Uke over the propped foot.

1.Tori retreats three steps, drawing Uke to take a heavy forward step with the right foot.
2.Tori steps the left foot back-left, lifts the right lapel (Tsurite), and pulls the left sleeve (Hikite).
3.Tori props the sole of the right foot against Uke's right ankle, wheeling Uke over the barrier.
Kodokan Key Point: Ankle prop — block the ankle firmly while lifting high with the Tsurite.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaAnkle Prop
04
浮腰 Uki-goshi "Floating Hip"

Tori steps inside Uke's attack, wraps the right arm around Uke's waist, inserts the hip half-way, and rotates the torso rapidly to float Uke over the hip.

1.Tori steps forward-left inside Uke's space as Uke advances.
2.Tori slides the right arm tightly around Uke's waist, fitting the right hip against Uke's lower abdomen (shallow half-hip turn).
3.Tori twists the chest and hips sharply without bending over, whipping Uke in a lateral spinning arc.
Kodokan Key Point: Torsional hip twist — quick shallow hip insertion with rapid upper body twist.
Dai-ikkyoKoshi-wazaFloating Hip
05
大外刈 Osoto-gari "Major Outer Reap"

Tori steps deep alongside Uke's right foot, pulls Uke's weight onto the right heel, swings the right leg high, and reaps the back of Uke's right knee with the back of the thigh.

1.Tori steps the left foot deep beside Uke's right foot, pulling the sleeve down and pushing the lapel back.
2.Tori presses chest to chest, swinging the right leg forward and upward like a pendulum.
3.Tori drives forward, reaping Uke's right leg backward from behind the knee.
Kodokan Key Point: Heel lock & chest drive — ensure Uke's weight is pinned to the heel before executing the pendulum reap.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaOuter Reap
06
大腰 O-goshi "Major Hip Throw"

Tori pulls Uke forward onto the toes, wraps the right arm around Uke's waist, lowers the hips deep beneath Uke's belt, and lifts Uke across the hip fulcrum.

1.Tori pulls Uke forward-upward onto the toes with the left sleeve.
2.Tori slides the right arm around Uke's waist, pivots 180 degrees, and bends both knees deeply beneath Uke's center of mass.
3.Tori extends the knees, rolls Uke over the hip, and projects Uke forward to the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Deep hip lowering — bend the knees so Tori's belt line is lower than Uke's belt before lifting.
Dai-ikkyoKoshi-wazaMajor Hip
07
大内刈 Ouchi-gari "Major Inner Reap"

Tori breaks Uke's balance to the rear-left corner, steps inside, hooks the right leg behind Uke's left inner thigh/knee, and reaps outward in a wide circular arc.

1.Tori spreads Uke's base, driving Uke's weight back onto the left heel.
2.Tori steps the left foot close to Uke's center and hooks the right leg deeply between Uke's legs.
3.Tori reaps Uke's left leg outward along the mat while driving the upper body forward.
Kodokan Key Point: Inner calf reap — hook the calf and reap outward like drawing a circle on the mat.
Dai-ikkyoAshi-wazaInner Reap
08
背負投 Seoi-nage "Shoulder Throw (Morote-seoi-nage)"

Tori pulls Uke forward onto the toes, pivots 180 degrees, wedges the right elbow deep into Uke's right armpit while maintaining the lapel grip, and throws Uke over the back.

1.Tori pulls the left sleeve forward and lifts the right lapel to break Uke's balance forward.
2.Tori pivots 180 degrees, wedging the right flexed elbow into Uke's right armpit with bent knees.
3.Tori extends the legs, bends forward at the waist, and throws Uke cleanly over the shoulder.
Kodokan Key Point: Elbow wedge — wedge the right elbow securely into the armpit while pulling the sleeve across.
Dai-ikkyoTe-wazaShoulder Throw
09
小外刈Kosoto-gari"Minor Outer Reap"

Tori breaks Uke's balance directly onto the right heel, steps outside, and reaps the back of Uke's right heel with the sole of the left foot.

1.Tori drives Uke backward onto the right heel.
2.Tori steps the right foot outside Uke's left foot and clips the back of Uke's right heel with the left foot.
3.Tori pushes with the upper body to complete the reap.
Kodokan Key Point: Heel clip — clip the heel from the outside while driving backward.
Dai-nikyoAshi-wazaOuter Heel Reap
10
小内刈Kouchi-gari"Minor Inner Reap"

Tori drives Uke's balance onto the right heel, slides the right foot inside, and reaps Uke's right heel from the inside with the sole of the foot.

1.Tori pulls and pushes to fix Uke's weight on the right heel.
2.Tori slides the right foot along the tatami, hooking the inside of Uke's right heel with the sole.
3.Tori reaps the heel forward while driving the upper body forward.
Kodokan Key Point: Inner heel scoop — sweep the heel forward along the tatami like a spoon.
Dai-nikyoAshi-wazaInner Heel Reap
11
腰車Koshi-guruma"Hip Wheel"

Tori pulls Uke forward onto the toes, wraps the right arm around the back of Uke's neck, inserts the hip deeply, and wheels Uke over the hip.

1.Tori breaks Uke's balance forward.
2.Tori wraps the right arm around Uke's neck, pivots 180 degrees, and blocks Uke's thighs with the hips.
3.Tori bows forward, wheeling Uke over the hip fulcrum.
Kodokan Key Point: Head wrap — control the cervical spine with the arm around the neck while wheeling.
Dai-nikyoKoshi-wazaHip Wheel
12
釣込腰Tsurikomi-goshi"Lifting Hip Throw"

Tori lifts high with the right lapel (Tsurite) to break Uke's defensive posture, drops the hips deeply beneath Uke's belt, and springs Uke over the hips.

1.Tori pushes the right collar upward like lifting an umbrella to float Uke.
2.Tori pivots 180 degrees, dropping the hips low between Uke's legs.
3.Tori extends the legs and throws Uke cleanly forward.
Kodokan Key Point: High Tsurite lift — lift with the collar wrist before turning the hips in.
Dai-nikyoKoshi-wazaLifting Hip
13
送足払Okuri-ashi-harai"Sliding Foot Sweep"

Moving sideways across the tatami in Tsugi-ashi, Tori sweeps Uke's trailing ankle into the leading ankle as Uke brings both feet together.

1.Both move laterally across the mat in rapid sideways sliding steps.
2.Tori lifts Uke slightly to create weightlessness.
3.Tori sweeps the trailing foot into the lead foot with the sole of the foot.
Kodokan Key Point: Dual ankle sweep — sweep both feet together when Uke is unweighted between steps.
Dai-nikyoAshi-wazaSliding Sweep
14
体落Tai-otoshi"Body Drop"

Tori pulls Uke forward-right onto the toes, pivots, extends the right leg across Uke's right shin without hip contact, and drives with the hands to drop Uke over the extended leg.

1.Tori pulls with both hands in a wide horizontal steering motion.
2.Tori pivots and places the right leg wide across Uke's right shin/ankle.
3.Tori rotates the chest and drives downward with the hands to throw Uke.
Kodokan Key Point: Hand throw mechanics — no hip contact; the throw relies on hand drive over the extended leg barrier.
Dai-nikyoTe-wazaBody Drop
15
払腰Harai-goshi"Sweeping Hip Throw"

Tori draws Uke forward onto the toes, inserts the right hip, and sweeps the right leg backward along the outside of Uke's right thigh while rotating the chest.

1.Tori pulls Uke forward onto the toes in a circular step.
2.Tori inserts the hip and plants the left support foot close to Uke's center.
3.Tori sweeps the right leg back against Uke's thigh to complete the throw.
Kodokan Key Point: Thigh sweep & chest turn — coordinate the leg sweep and upper body turn simultaneously.
Dai-nikyoKoshi-wazaSweeping Hip
16
内股Uchi-mata"Inner Thigh Throw"

Tori draws Uke forward in a circle, pivots 180 degrees, plants the support foot between Uke's feet, and kicks the right leg high up between Uke's thighs to reap the inner thigh.

1.Tori breaks Uke's balance forward onto the toes.
2.Tori plants the left support foot directly between Uke's feet with high lapel lift.
3.Tori kicks the right leg up between Uke's thighs, lifting Uke's inner thigh to throw.
Kodokan Key Point: Central reaping kick — drive the head down and kick the reaping leg high between Uke's thighs.
Dai-nikyoAshi-wazaInner Thigh Throw
17
小外掛Kosoto-gake"Minor Outer Hook"

Tori breaks Uke's balance to the rear-right corner, hooks the right leg behind Uke's right ankle/calf, and drives forward to hook Uke down.

1.Tori drives Uke backward onto the right heel.
2.Tori steps in and wraps the left leg behind Uke's right calf/ankle.
3.Tori drives forward with the chest, hooking the leg to take Uke down.
Kodokan Key Point: Leg hook & forward drive — hook the leg firmly while driving bodyweight forward.
Dai-sankyoAshi-wazaOuter Hook
18
釣腰Tsuri-goshi"Lifting Hip Throw (Belt Grip)"

Tori reaches over or under Uke's arm to grip the rear belt, bends the knees, and hoists Uke over the hip by lifting directly on the belt.

1.Tori reaches deep over Uke's shoulder to grip the back of Uke's belt.
2.Tori pivots, inserts the hips low, and pulls upward on the belt.
3.Tori extends the legs and rolls Uke over the hips.
Kodokan Key Point: Belt hoist — lift directly with the belt grip to hoist Uke onto the hip fulcrum.
Dai-sankyoKoshi-wazaBelt Hip Throw
19
横落Yoko-otoshi"Side Drop"

Tori breaks Uke's balance directly to the right side, slides the left leg out wide across Uke's feet, and drops sideways to the mat to pull Uke down.

1.Tori pulls Uke's balance directly sideways onto the right foot.
2.Tori slides the left leg flat along the tatami outside Uke's right foot.
3.Tori sacrifices posture to the side, dropping Uke over the extended leg.
Kodokan Key Point: Direct lateral drop — drop straight sideways to the mat while extending the leg barrier.
Dai-sankyoYoko-sutemiSide Drop
20
足車Ashi-guruma"Leg Wheel"

Tori pulls Uke forward-right, pivots, extends the right leg horizontally across both of Uke's shins, and wheels Uke forward over the leg.

1.Tori pulls Uke forward onto the toes in a wide arc.
2.Tori pivots and extends the right leg horizontally across Uke's lower shins.
3.Tori rotates the upper body to wheel Uke over the leg barrier without hip contact.
Kodokan Key Point: Shin barrier — extend the leg across the shins below the knee as a rotating fulcrum.
Dai-sankyoAshi-wazaLeg Wheel
21
跳腰Hane-goshi"Spring Hip Throw"

Tori pulls Uke forward onto the toes, inserts the hip, bends the right knee, and springs upward with the hip and outer leg against Uke's inner thigh.

1.Tori breaks Uke's balance forward-right onto the toes.
2.Tori pivots, fitting the right hip and bent right leg against Uke's inner thigh.
3.Tori springs upward off the left support leg, launching Uke over the hip.
Kodokan Key Point: Springing leg & hip — spring upward with the bent leg and hip acting as a unified elevator.
Dai-sankyoKoshi-wazaSpring Hip
22
払釣込足Harai-tsurikomi-ashi"Lifting Pulling Foot Sweep"

Tori retreats, lifts high with the Tsurite, and sweeps Uke's advancing ankle backward with the sole of the foot as Uke steps forward.

1.Tori retreats, lifting Uke's lapel high and pulling the sleeve.
2.As Uke steps forward, Tori applies the sole of the foot to Uke's ankle.
3.Tori sweeps the ankle backward while continuing the high lift with the hands.
Kodokan Key Point: Lifting foot sweep — sweep the foot back while maintaining a strong upward lift with the collar.
Dai-sankyoAshi-wazaLifting Sweep
23
巴投Tomoe-nage"Circular Stomach Throw"

Tori steps deep between Uke's feet, sits close to Uke's heels, places the sole of the foot on Uke's abdomen, and rolls backward to flip Uke overhead.

1.Tori pulls Uke forward in a defensive stance.
2.Tori slides the support foot deep between Uke's feet and sits down close to the heels.
3.Tori places the foot on Uke's abdomen, rolls onto the back, and projects Uke overhead.
Kodokan Key Point: Deep sitting drop — sit as close to Uke's heels as possible before rolling backward.
Dai-sankyoMa-sutemiStomach Throw
24
肩車Kata-guruma"Shoulder Wheel"

Tori breaks Uke's balance forward, drops hips deeply, slides the right arm between Uke's thighs, hoists Uke across both shoulders, and wheels Uke to the mat.

1.Tori pulls Uke forward onto the toes.
2.Tori bends knees deeply, sliding the arm between Uke's thighs onto the shoulders.
3.Tori stands up, lifting Uke across the neck, and wheels Uke forward-left to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Deep knee bend — lower the center of gravity below Uke's hips before hoisting.
Dai-sankyoTe-wazaShoulder Wheel
25
隅返Sumi-gaeshi"Corner Reversal"

From a bent posture, Tori grips the rear belt, drops backward, hooks the instep inside Uke's thigh, and flips Uke diagonally over the corner.

1.Tori grips Uke's back belt over the shoulder in Jigotai.
2.Tori sits backward, sliding the right instep under Uke's inner thigh.
3.Tori lifts with the instep and pulls the belt to flip Uke over the diagonal corner.
Kodokan Key Point: Instep hook & corner flip — lift the inner thigh with the instep toward Uke's unweighted corner.
Dai-yonkyoMa-sutemiCorner Reversal
26
谷落Tani-otoshi"Valley Drop"

Tori breaks Uke's balance backward, steps deep behind both of Uke's heels, slides the leg across, and drops straight down to pull Uke down.

1.Tori pulls Uke's weight back onto the heels.
2.Tori steps behind Uke's feet, extending the leg across Uke's calves.
3.Tori drops bodyweight to the tatami, pulling Uke backward into the drop.
Kodokan Key Point: Rear leg slide — slide the leg behind both heels without stamping on Uke's knee.
Dai-yonkyoYoko-sutemiValley Drop
27
跳巻込Hane-makikomi"Springing Wraparound"

Tori enters as if for Hane-goshi, wraps Uke's right arm under the armpit, and throws the body into a continuous rolling sacrifice to wrap Uke over.

1.Tori enters for Hane-goshi with forward unbalance.
2.Tori clamps Uke's right arm tightly under the right armpit.
3.Tori wraps the torso around and falls forward-right, driving Uke into a rolling fall.
Kodokan Key Point: Armpit clamp & roll — clamp the arm tightly and commit bodyweight into the wraparound roll.
Dai-yonkyoYoko-sutemiWraparound Throw
28
掬投Sukui-nage"Scooping Throw"

Tori steps behind Uke's flank, scoops both thighs or knees from behind with the hands, and drives forward to dump Uke backward onto the mat.

1.Tori evades Uke's attack, moving to Uke's rear flank.
2.Tori reaches both hands behind Uke's thighs, scooping the legs upward.
3.Tori pushes Uke's chest/shoulder with bodyweight, dumping Uke backward.
Kodokan Key Point: Thigh scoop & body drive — scoop the thighs upward while driving the body forward.
Dai-yonkyoTe-wazaScooping Throw
29
移腰Utsuri-goshi"Shifting Hip Counter"

As Uke attacks with a hip throw, Tori blocks with the abdomen, hoists Uke off the ground, shifts Uke onto Tori's hip, and throws Uke over the hip.

1.Uke enters for a hip throw; Tori bends knees and blocks with the pelvis.
2.Tori lifts Uke off the mat using the legs and wraps the arm around Uke's waist.
3.Tori shifts Uke onto the forward hip and rolls Uke to the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Hip shift counter — hoist Uke into the air before transferring Uke onto Tori's hip.
Dai-yonkyoKoshi-wazaHip Counter
30
大車O-guruma"Major Wheel"

Tori pulls Uke forward onto the toes, pivots, extends the right leg horizontally across Uke's lower abdomen/thighs, and wheels Uke over the leg fulcrum.

1.Tori breaks Uke's balance forward onto the toes in a wide arc.
2.Tori pivots and places the right leg horizontally across Uke's upper thighs/hips.
3.Tori rotates the chest downward, wheeling Uke over the high leg fulcrum.
Kodokan Key Point: High thigh fulcrum — position the leg horizontally across Uke's hips/upper thighs rather than the lower shins.
Dai-yonkyoAshi-wazaMajor Wheel
31
外巻込Soto-makikomi"Outer Wraparound"

Tori enters for a hip throw, clamps Uke's right arm tightly under the right armpit, and throws the body into a full forward-right wraparound sacrifice.

1.Tori pulls Uke forward onto the toes.
2.Tori pivots, clamping Uke's right arm under the right armpit.
3.Tori drives bodyweight forward-right onto the mat, wrapping Uke over in a rolling throw.
Kodokan Key Point: Full body wraparound — commit bodyweight into the forward roll while clamping the arm.
Dai-yonkyoYoko-sutemiOuter Wraparound
32
浮落Uki-otoshi"Floating Drop"

Tori retreats in Tsugi-ashi, breaks Uke's balance forward-right, drops the left knee to the tatami without body contact, and pulls Uke down in a circular arc.

1.Tori retreats three steps, drawing Uke to push forward.
2.Tori drops the left knee straight to the tatami.
3.Tori pulls both hands downward and backward in a large circular arc to project Uke.
Kodokan Key Point: No body contact — project Uke purely through hand pull, body drop, and timing.
Dai-yonkyoTe-wazaFloating Drop
33
大外車Osoto-guruma"Major Outer Wheel"

Tori breaks Uke's balance onto both heels, extends the right leg across the back of both of Uke's knees, and wheels Uke backward over the leg barrier.

1.Tori drives Uke's weight back onto both heels.
2.Tori steps the left foot deep and extends the right leg horizontally behind both of Uke's knees.
3.Tori drives the chest forward, wheeling Uke backward over the leg.
Kodokan Key Point: Dual knee barrier — block behind both of Uke's legs simultaneously while wheeling backward.
Dai-gokyoAshi-wazaOuter Wheel
34
浮技Uki-waza"Floating Technique"

Uke pushes in deep Jigotai. Tori retreats, steps wide, breaks Uke's balance to the forward-right corner, and drops onto the left side to float Uke over in a forward roll.

1.Tori retreats against Uke's pushing pressure in Jigotai.
2.Tori steps the left foot wide to the side and pulls Uke across in a diagonal arc.
3.Tori drops onto the left side on the mat, pulling Uke into a floating forward roll.
Kodokan Key Point: Diagonal side sacrifice — drop onto the side to create a deep diagonal void for Uke's roll.
Dai-gokyoYoko-sutemiFloating Technique
35
横分Yoko-wakare"Side Separation"

Uke pushes forward. Tori steps directly between Uke's feet, drops flat onto the side across Uke's line of advance, and projects Uke forward over Tori's body.

1.Uke pushes forward with strong momentum.
2.Tori steps directly in front of Uke's feet and drops bodyweight sideways flat to the mat.
3.Tori pulls both arms vigorously to separate and throw Uke forward over the body.
Kodokan Key Point: Direct perpendicular drop — drop flat across Uke's path to separate Uke from their base.
Dai-gokyoYoko-sutemiSide Separation
36
横車Yoko-guruma"Side Wheel"

As Uke bends forward defensively to resist a rear throw, Tori drops onto the side, slides the right leg deep between Uke's thighs, and wheels Uke diagonally over.

1.Tori wraps Uke's waist; Uke bends forward to resist Ura-nage.
2.Tori slides the right leg between Uke's thighs and falls onto the left hip.
3.Tori wheels Uke in a large diagonal circle over the body.
Kodokan Key Point: Leg insertion & wheel — slide the leg between Uke's thighs while wheeling sideways.
Dai-gokyoYoko-sutemiSide Wheel
37
後腰Ushiro-goshi"Rear Hip Counter"

As Uke enters for a hip or shoulder throw, Tori lowers hips, wraps both arms around Uke's waist from behind, hoists Uke straight up, and drops backward to mat.

1.Uke enters for a forward throw.
2.Tori sinks hips, clamps Uke's waist from behind, and arches to hoist Uke into the air.
3.Tori pushes Uke forward and drops backward, dumping Uke flat on the mat.
Kodokan Key Point: Rear hip hoist — lift Uke straight up from behind using hip drive before dumping.
Dai-gokyoKoshi-wazaRear Counter
38
裏投Ura-nage"Rear Throw / Suplex"

Tori steps deep behind Uke's side, wraps both arms around Uke's torso, drops weight backward, thrusts the hips into a full bridge, and throws Uke over the shoulder.

1.Tori steps behind Uke's side and wraps both arms around the waist/torso.
2.Tori drops backward, arching the back and driving the hips upward into a high bridge.
3.Tori throws Uke backward over the left shoulder onto the tatami.
Kodokan Key Point: Pelvic extension & bridge — drive the pelvis upward explosively to launch Uke safely over the shoulder.
Dai-gokyoMa-sutemiRear Suplex
39
隅落Sumi-otoshi"Corner Drop"

Tori draws Uke into rear corner unbalance, steps in close without leg or hip contact, and uses pure hand coordination to drop Uke over the rear-right corner.

1.Tori maneuvers Uke so all weight shifts onto the right heel.

The 40th and final technique of the Gokyo-no-Waza. Tori draws Uke onto the right heel, hooks the outside of Uke's right ankle with the sole of the foot, and drops sideways to the mat.

1.Tori retreats in Ayumi-ashi, drawing Uke to commit full weight onto the right heel.
2.Tori applies the sole of the left foot to the outside of Uke's right ankle.
3.Tori drops bodyweight flat to the left side, sweeping Uke down to complete the 40 techniques.
Kodokan Key Point: Synchronized side drop — the lateral body drop and ankle sweep occur in one instantaneous motion.

Pedagogy & Structure

Kodokan Gokyo Pedagogical Structure

The Gokyo-no-Waza represents the foundational classification that underpins all Kodokan standing throwing technique.

Evolution (1895 & 1920)

Established in 1895 with 42 throws, the Kodokan revised the system in 1920 to 40 throws (removing 8 dangerous/redundant techniques and adding 6 modern variations) to maximize safety and pedagogical progression.

Biomechanical Balance

The 40 throws encompass all major throwing categories: Hand (Te-waza), Hip (Koshi-waza), Foot/Leg (Ashi-waza), Rear Sacrifice (Ma-sutemi-waza), and Side Sacrifice (Yoko-sutemi-waza).

Kata Performance (Go-Kyo-no-Kata)

When demonstrated as a formal kata, techniques are performed in standard Gokyo sequence with formal bows (Ritsurei and Zarei), demonstrating fluid transitions and bilateral balance control.
